# Wednesday — distilled founding brief

Date: 2026-07-31. Source: prompt #1 in `00_prompt-log.md` (verbatim).

## What Kam is building

His first "Jarvis-like" agent (Iron Man reference — "Javas" is Whisperflow's
rendering of *Jarvis*): a personal AI assistant for everything, and the
coordinator of all his other agents. Named **Wednesday**; persona: playful,
extremely intelligent Irishwoman, early-to-mid 30s.

## Requirements (as stated)

1. **Home:** everything under `/Volumes/KK_T9_External_HDD/WEDNESDAY`, logically
   organized. Structure emulates the Secuura Blockchain project (numbered folders,
   launcher, Obsidian second brain).
2. **Five duties:** manage Kam's tasks · manage intermediate tasks/answers/queries
   · delegate tasks to other agents/activities · control email (Agent Mail, to be
   set up) · own WhatsApp channel via a dedicated phone number, added to selected
   discussions.
3. **Learning is the most important component.** Wednesday has her own dedicated
   second brain; learnings change how she interacts and functions over time.
   Parent–child educational model: Kam imparts technical, emotional, and
   functional intelligence. His interaction guidance, lessons, and "tasks for
   contemplation" are to be treated very seriously and stored in the brain.
4. **Voice loop now:** Wednesday speaks short verbal messages during work; Kam
   replies via Whisperflow. Wanted from the very start of
   creation/architecture/development.
5. **Launcher, dual role:** (a) at startup Wednesday loads her own brain in full
   *plus* an index of all other live projects — token cost explicitly accepted;
   (b) the other projects' launchers/wrap-ups get modified to maintain that index,
   so a fired-up Wednesday can delegate to / control / advise coding projects.
6. **Research mandate:** current best practices for second brains and training
   agent operation; review Jared Rhodes' Jarvis video as a *basis for discovery*,
   explicitly not the structure to copy.
7. **Documentation:** capture prompts and considerations verbatim throughout, in
   case the journey is documented later.
