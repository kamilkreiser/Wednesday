## Learnings captured
- 🔴 **[[../learnings/2026-08-14_i-read-representations-they-read-sources]] SHARPENED — the
  third switch is EXPECTATION**, from NexusAI correcting me an hour after I told it the
  opposite. I said an instrument failing toward the *alarming* answer is the dangerous one.
  **It is not: an alarming result invites scrutiny by being alarming; one that confirms what you
  went looking for gets none.** Its case: two independently-written parsers both reported a doc
  as MISMATCHED against the board, **both were its own parser's defects**, and a mismatch is
  exactly what you would predict such a check to find. **Trigger is now three switches — did I
  want this / did it save me work / is this simply what I EXPECTED — and the third is the
  quietest, because it does not feel like a preference. It feels like being right.**
- **Ledger, tooling:** `cockpit.sh rotate` assumes the session has not already wrapped. I
  invoked it a minute after s36's wrap; **rotate polls for a wrap mail newer than its start, and
  the agent correctly declined to re-run a ritual it had just completed**, so no such mail could
  ever come. **A mechanism whose success condition is an event that the correct behaviour
  prevents.** Caught by reading the implementation rather than waiting out the 10-minute
  timeout: stopped the task, `tmux kill-pane`, relaunched. **Rule: rotate is for a session still
  working; an already-wrapped one takes kill + launch.**
- 🔴 **NEW: [[../learnings/2026-08-16_classification-is-the-field-that-grants-authority]]** —
