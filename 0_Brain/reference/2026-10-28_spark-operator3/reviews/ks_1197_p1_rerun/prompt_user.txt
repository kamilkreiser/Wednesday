===== TICKET =====
KS-1197 — A non-string verificationLevel claim: POST /api/documents answers 500 and a verifier-gated verify never answers (async throw) [Backlog]

## BLUF

A token whose `verificationLevel` claim is **not a string** (a number or an array) breaks both callers of the gateway's level check, identically on base and on #1014:

* `POST /api/documents` answers **500** (the KS-529 wrapper catches the `TypeError` from `.toLowerCase()`).
* `POST /api/documents/:id/verify` on a verifier-gated type **never answers**. The async handler throws at `routes/verification.ts:556-557`, the rejection is unhandled, and the socket hangs.

## Recommendation

Not built. Low reach: it needs an auth-signed token with a non-string level, and auth mints enum strings only (`userRepo.ts` maps unknown levels to `BASIC`), so it is not client-forgeable. The fix shape: coerce or validate the claim once where the principal is built (`middleware/auth.ts`), and make the verify handler's rejection reach the error handler.
