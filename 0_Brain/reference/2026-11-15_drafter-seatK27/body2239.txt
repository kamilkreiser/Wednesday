## What this admits

- An `ORG_ADMIN` whose token's tenant claim equals the tenant the certification was looked up in may share it and recertify it, even when that admin is neither its issuer nor its holder.
- **Tenant-wide, sibling organisations included:** an ORG_ADMIN of organisation B shares and recertifies a certification issued in organisation A of the same tenant. A recertify by that admin saves a new certificate naming the admin as its issuer and writes a `verification_certificate` charge; a share sends the email and writes a `share` charge.
- Not admitted: an ORG_ADMIN of another tenant (refused at the lookup, and by the claim if the two ever differ), an ORG_ADMIN with no tenant claim, and every other role, including `ISSUER_ADMIN`, `ADMIN`, `USER` and the old `platform_admin` spelling.
- A possible later refinement, not built here: bind the admit to the admin's own organisation (`decideTenantAccess`'s organisation-scope arm). The certification row exposes no organisation, so that is not a one-line change.
