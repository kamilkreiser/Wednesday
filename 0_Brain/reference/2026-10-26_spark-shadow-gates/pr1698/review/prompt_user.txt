## Checklist

- [ ] **m1, a stalled response BODY is outside every timer, in ANY window, and the code comment overclaims.** `fetchWithTimeout` (`platform.ts:143-158`) clears its timer when `fetch()` resolves, which is at the response HEADERS; `await r.json()` (`:416`) then runs outside any timer. Measured by the gate (budget 2000 ms, a stub holding the body back after flushing headers): a stalled 8 s body in a **non-last** window gave **8014 ms**, in the **last** window **8006 ms**. Same as the old route's one request, not worse (the worst case is one stall, not 26). The comment at `:401` ("never hangs") is true for slow responses and false for a stalled body. *Fix shape:* bound the body read by the remaining budget (keep the `AbortController` live until after `r.json()`, or race the body read against a `remaining` timer), or at minimum soften the comment to "slow responses" and record the caveat. *Cell to add:* a stalled body in a NON-last window degrades within budget + margin.
- [ ] **m2, the guard "no window starts once nothing is left" (**`platform.ts:407`**) is unpinned.** The gate's mutant that removes it survives **all 18 cells**; with it removed the stub sees a second request that is aborted at once. *Fix shape:* in cell M assert `calls.length === 1` (the stub already records calls).
- [ ] **m3, a final window that answers AFTER the deadline keeps the platform half and returns late.** Measured: a last-window body at 1.5 times the budget returned in **3021 ms** (budget 2000 ms) with the platform rows served. Wednesday's ruling said a spent budget DISCARDS the platform half; today the discard applies only when another window would be needed. She accepted this for the merge because it is bounded by the same body-stall exposure as the old route. *Fix shape:* one deadline check after the body is read (`if (Date.now() >= deadline) throw`), plus a cell. Note it would not bound the time (see m1); it only stops a late result from being served as if on time. A ruling-interpretation question as well as a fix: the rows served are complete, never partial.
- [ ] **nit, cell G's second assertion cannot fail** (`ks1512-platform-audit-log-pages-the-upstream-past-its-200-clamp.test.ts`, the `calls.every(... || c.offset === 200 || c.offset === 100 || c.offset === 0)` line, around `:214`): any plausible offset satisfies it. The first assertion (the ids equal the oracle) is the real check. Delete the second or make it real.
