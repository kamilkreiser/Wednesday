## What this test can and cannot support

- Every sampled row is a mistake made by a seat that HAD read the full digest. The replay therefore finds places where the card says less than the lesson on a known failure. It cannot find a mistake the digest's extra text prevented, because a prevented mistake leaves no ledger row. "Nothing that matters is lost" is not a claim this method can reach; a forward trial is needed (a seat boots on the card while the digest stays readable, and its ledger rows are compared with a digest-boot seat's over the same days).
- Grades are one reader's judgement of whether a line "states what to do". Many COVERED grades rest on a general line (the same general rule the lesson gives); I did not count how many. There was no second grader.

## 2. Grades used

- **COVERED**: a line on the seat's card states the needed behaviour.
- **COVERED-ENFORCED**: a tool or hook refuses this instance. I opened the file and name the lines. Only 2 index rows are still flagged as enforced, so most of these are hooks with no index row, or rows whose flag the review removed.
- **PARTIAL**: a line is related; the clause that mattered is in the lesson (so in the digest) and not in the line.
- **MISSING**: no line, or a line filed as tool-enforced where the tool would not have refused.
- **LEDGER-ONLY** (added by me): the card line says as much as the lesson; the specific rule exists only in the ledger row. Not a loss against the digest.
- **NO-LESSON**: the row cites no lesson and no rule exists in any lesson or index row.
