# CoAgent: the deputy, "your next change settles it" (design note for the ROSTER-SETTLE build)

Written 2026-11-20 for the coordinator. Ruling built: card `general-c2c-deputy-d2-settle-on-change-1120`, 2026-11-20 11:43:18, verbatim option "a — Your next change settles it" (relayed by the coordinator; I did not find the board record in a file, so the quote is UNVERIFIED at source). Everything below was read at code main `32beb563837341560b908d9ba5b0a60de240d860` with `git show`; `deputy.rs`, `lib.rs`, `intake.rs`, `deputy_intake.rs` are under `crates/coagent-roster/src/`. Nothing here was run: every claim about behaviour is READ or REASONED, and marked.

**Is option a sound as the card states it? As the owner sees it, yes. As a build ("smallest build: keeping already exists; one rule changes", doc 13 §3), no.** If only the starter's helper settles, the D-2 failure stays reachable by honest parties, because the starter's computer can change the member list before it has heard of an admission (the deputy acts exactly while the starter is out of reach), and that admission then stands at the members who hold it (§1.3). And "drop this person but leave the deputy's seats as they are" cannot be written as one version at `32beb56` at all (§1.4). **Smallest sound variant (called A2 below, and the one the brief builds): every member list the starter signs starts a new epoch for the deputy's seats, and every member's validator enforces that.** The person-level behaviour is the card's, with one addition the owner may notice (§5, O-1).

## 1. The rule today, and why G3 and G4 fail

1. **A carried grant keeps its epoch** (design 06 §2.8.2 rule 3, line 313). Code: `check_grant_transition`, `deputy.rs:154-176`: same holder, seats and `cap` → the epoch must equal the old one, else `GrantEpochChanged` (`:165-171`); otherwise it must be the new version's number, else `GrantEpochNotNew` (`:172-174`). One version alone: epoch in `1..=version` (`deputy.rs:146-148`). Docs: `docs/ROSTER.md:477`.
2. **Admissions stand across a starter version that keeps the epoch** (design 06 line 346; `docs/ROSTER.md:414-416`). Code: `SessionChain::apply_roster` voids only when the epoch differs or the grant is gone (`deputy.rs:1131-1135`).
3. **Rule 6: the proposal names a version "at or after `epoch` that this member holds"** (design 06 line 339). Code: `deputy.rs:1411-1416` (`ProposalBeforeEpoch`, then `held(p.at_version) != Some(p.at_hash.0)` → `ProposalVersionNotHeld`); `held_hash` reads this member's own stored versions (`deputy.rs:1241-1246`). A member that started later holds only versions from its start (`RosterChain::start`, `lib.rs:898-899`; `start_provisional`, `deputy.rs:1019-1020`).
4. **G3** (the check's probe, `m1_review/gate_deputy_tests/qa_deputy_gate.rs:94-128`): epoch 3; admission a0 names version 3; the starter's version 4 changes a role and carries epoch 3; a1 names version 4. The newcomer is handed version 4 and [a0, a1]; a0 names version 3, which it does not hold → `Admission { index: 0, error: ProposalVersionNotHeld }`. **G4** (`:133-146`): a member invited directly in version 4 holds only version 4 and refuses a0 the same way; the old member keeps a0, so the two view hashes differ and their handshake fails.
5. **Helper-only settling does not close it (REASONED).** Deputy signs a0 at version 3; the starter has not received it; the starter's helper sees nobody to settle and signs version 4 with the grant carried (epoch 3, as rule 3 demands). Members holding a0 keep it. The next newcomer is in G3's position. No party misbehaved.
6. **"Drop, same seats" is not expressible today (READ).** Dropping needs a new epoch; the same holder and seats may not change epoch (`GrantEpochChanged`); `next_with_deputy` refuses the grant already in force (`GrantUnchanged`, `deputy.rs:310-317`). The builder recorded this as design question 3 (STATUS_M3_DEPUTY line 76: "Renewing an identical grant needs two versions").

## 2. The new rule (variant A2)

**R-1, every member's validator.** A version that carries a grant carries `epoch` equal to its own version number. This REPLACES design 06 §2.8.2 rule 3 and the first sentence of "When the starter's chain moves" (line 346). It is a rule of one version alone, so a joiner checks it on its first member list too. Consequence at every member, with `apply_roster` unchanged (`deputy.rs:1131-1135`): every accepted starter version has a new epoch (or no grant), so every admission held is void; whoever is not on the new member list is reported in `SessionReport::dropped`.

**R-2, the starter's helper (an honest starter).** Every provisional member the starter's computer knows of when it builds a version is either KEPT (a member entry in that version, in the seat it held, with its admitted role) or DROPPED (its key id added to `dropped_keys`). The default is keep all.

**R-3, the grant after settling.** The grant stands with the same holder; its seats are the old seats minus those the kept members now sit in (a member may not sit in a set-aside seat, `GrantSlotOccupied`, `deputy.rs:142-144`); the seats of dropped people are free again and stay set aside; the epoch is the new version's number. If no seat remains, the entry carries no grant (design 06 I-21: the role lasts "until the granted seats are used up"). The same epoch cannot stand with seats freed: the deputy's chain is identified by (session, epoch, seq), so restarting `seq` under one epoch would make two honest admissions look like the deputy's two stories (`AdmissionEvidence::verify`, `deputy.rs:712-734`). That is why a NEW epoch is needed, and why "members just discard on every version, epoch unchanged" was rejected.

**What a member can and cannot check.**
- Checkable by all: no admission outlives a starter version (R-1); a kept person is an ordinary member entry (the existing member rules).
- Not checkable: that the starter recorded every drop. Members validate the member list without the admissions (`docs/ROSTER.md:429-431`); this is as built today.
- A dishonest starter who skips the helper gains nothing: carrying an old epoch is refused by every member (R-1); not recording a drop only lets the deputy admit that key again, which equals the starter inviting it himself; "keeping" someone the deputy never admitted is a direct invite. REASONED.
- Rejected: members REFUSE a starter version that leaves admissions standing. That would let the deputy block the starter by sending an admission first, against design 06 §2.8.6 ("Block, override or outrun a change by the starter: Cannot") and the storage rule of the deputy brief §5.3.

## 3. Why G3 and G4 pass afterwards (REASONED, from the rules)

Let C be the starter's current version, number N, carrying a grant.
1. By R-1 the grant's epoch is N.
2. An admission is accepted against C only if its epoch is N (rule 1, `deputy.rs:1272-1277`).
3. Rule 6 then requires `at_version ≥ N` (`deputy.rs:1411-1413`) and a version this member holds with that number and hash (`:1414-1416`). No member holds a version above N, so `at_version = N` and `at_hash = hash(C)`.
4. Every party that applies an admission of epoch N holds C: an old member, a joiner whose first list is C, and a provisional newcomer handed C (`start_provisional` stores it, `deputy.rs:1019-1020`). So no accepted admission ever names a version its holder lacks.
5. When version N+1 arrives, every admission of epoch N is void everywhere (R-1 with `deputy.rs:1131-1149`). So "an earlier admission from before a starter change" does not exist for anyone.
G3 as ruled: the role change is a settled version (a0's person kept: a member in seat 5; grant = seat 6, epoch 4); a1 has `seq` 0 and names version 4; the newcomer starts. G4 as ruled: the later joiner and the old member hold the same version and no admission; equal hashes; a0 offered to either is refused `WrongEpoch { held: 4, got: 3 }`.

**A proposal signed before the change** names the old version. At the starter's intake it is `Stale` (existing rule 4 of design 06 §2.5). At the deputy's intake it is `Stale` (`deputy_intake.rs:207`, `:263-265`). Inside an admission it is refused `ProposalBeforeEpoch`. The proposer proposes again against the new list; nothing new is built for this. **An admission signed before the change** and delivered after it is refused `WrongEpoch`.

## 4. Consequences

1. **Validator.** `deputy.rs:146-148` gains "epoch below the version → `GrantEpochNotNew`"; `check_grant_transition` (`:154-176`) loses its "same grant" branch. `RosterError::GrantEpochChanged` (`lib.rs:401-402`) is no longer returned; the variant stays (removing a public variant is a signature change). The red control `grant_epoch_unchecked` (`deputy.rs:158-161`) must switch off both lines to stay red on its text.
2. **`set_grant`** (`deputy.rs:254-279`): the "carried" epoch (`:265-268`) goes; the epoch is always the new version's number.
3. **`next_with_deputy`** (`deputy.rs:299-324`): signature and meaning unchanged (set, move or withdraw the grant; drops every provisional member; refuses the grant already in force). **`next_keeping`** (`:333-385`): unchanged.
4. **Plain changes have no helper today.** Invite, remove and role change are built by hand from a copy of the current body (the daemon does so for version 1, `crates/coagent-daemon/src/engine.rs:1756-1768`; the tests use a closure). With a grant standing, a copied body carries the old epoch and is refused. New: `next_plain(&ValidRoster)` (today's private `next_body_of`, `deputy.rs:281-289`, made public) and `settle(&MemberView, RosterBody, Option<&[KeyId]>)` (`None` = keep all), which applies R-2 and R-3 to ANY next body and runs the validator. With no grant and nobody provisional it returns the body unchanged, so every starter version can go through it.
5. **`next_with_cap`** (`lib.rs:515-532`): still refuses `GrantStands` (`:526-528`); it takes the roster only. With a grant standing the path is `next_plain`, set `cap`, `settle`. One version, no longer two.
6. **The starter's intake.** `ProposalIntake::approve_with_report` (`intake.rs:599-682`) copies the body (`:627`) and takes the roster only. With a grant standing its own validator run (`:643-656`) would refuse the copy and charge the proposer (`RefusedInvalid`). So: `approve`/`approve_with_report` refuse `IntakeError::GrantStands` before anything changes, and a new `approve_settling(view, id, role, added_at, now, keep)` settles first. A refusal caused by the starter's own keep choice must not cost the proposal (gate return F2, `intake.rs:593-598`).
7. **Kept members and the proposal record.** Design 06 line 346 says a kept entry carries no proposal "because the proposal named an older version and rule 2 of 2.4 would refuse it". Under R-1 that reason is gone: an admission of epoch N names version N and is kept in version N+1, which is exactly what rule 2.4 asks (`lib.rs:763`, `:1098-1102`). A kept entry COULD carry its record and pass the existing validator unchanged (REASONED; one known exception: two kept people whose proposals share an id, `DuplicateProposalId`). Not built here; see §5 C-3. With keep-all as the default, "no signed proposer" becomes the normal fate of everyone a deputy lets in, so this deserves a decision soon.
8. **The limit.** Keep-all cannot exceed it: members + set-aside seats ≤ `cap` in every version (`deputy.rs:134-136`), each provisional person sits in one set-aside seat, and keeping moves a seat from one side of that sum to the other. REASONED.
9. **The lifetime bound.** Keeping adds member entries; `members + removed` ≤ 32 (`lib.rs:658-662`) can refuse a keep. The starter then drops instead.
10. **`MAX_DROPPED_KEYS` = 32.** While a grant stands, `dropped + seats ≤ 32` (`deputy.rs:205-209`). Dropping d people and keeping the grant needs `dropped + d + remaining seats ≤ 32`, which can fail (25 dropped, 7 seats, drop all 7: 32 + 7). `settle` then returns `DroppedKeysNoRoom` and the starter withdraws the grant in the same version. **Drop all and withdraw always validates** below version 256: `d ≤ seats`, so `dropped + d ≤ 32`. The build must test this as an invariant (the starter is never stuck).
11. **`SessionReport`.** `grant_change` (`deputy.rs:1127-1130`) is now `Some` on every version that carries a grant (the epoch is part of `GrantInfo`). `dropped` holds both people the starter dropped and people his computer never saw; only the first are in `dropped_keys`. No signature changes; the meaning does, and the daemon lane must know.
12. **Evidence.** A pair verifies against the version whose number is its epoch (`deputy.rs:719-721`), which a member that was present holds. Unchanged code. D-3 (first come, 4 pairs per session) is unchanged and slightly more visible: after any starter version the kept pairs no longer verify against the CURRENT list.
13. **The deputy's intake, `start_provisional`, rule 6, the view, `admit_view`:** no code change needed for R-1 to R-3 (READ). Docs change: `docs/ROSTER.md:414-418`, `:477`, `:492`, `:495-496`, `:526-531`, `:570`, `:585`, `:588`, `:621-622`.
14. **Other crates.** `crates/coagent-audit` and `crates/coagent-daemon` import from this crate: `Applied, ForkEvidence, RosterChain, RosterError, SignedRoster, ValidRoster, body_hash, sign, Member, Removed, RosterBody, Role, CAP, MAX_ENTRIES_EVER, MAX_VERSIONS, admit` (`git grep coagent_roster` at `32beb56`). Counted uses outside the crate of `next_with_cap`, `next_with_deputy`, `next_keeping`, `ProposalIntake`, `MemberView`, `SessionChain`, `admit_view`, `DeputyIntake`: 0 each (control: `RosterError` 21). **No public signature changes.** Additions only: two functions, one method, two `IntakeError` variants. No existing roster without a grant validates, hashes or encodes differently.
15. **Design 06 itself** needs the same amendment (§2.8.2 rule 3; §2.8.3 line 346; "until the starter renews the grant" in §2.8.5, §2.8.6, §2.8.10). The builder edits `docs/ROSTER.md` only.

## 5. What stays undecided, and who decides

**The owner (a person would notice):**
- **O-1. Someone let in a moment before your change.** If the deputy let a person in and your computer had not yet heard of it when you changed the list, that person is put out by your change without being shown to you, is not recorded as dropped, and can be let in again. Inherent in R-1; the alternative is the unsound helper-only build.
- **O-2. "You are shown the people … and keep or drop each"** is a console and daemon behaviour that does not exist. This build is the crate rule and the helper only, with keep-all as the helper's default.
- **O-3. Keep-all can use up the deputy's seats,** after which nobody is deputy until you set seats aside again.

**The coordinator (build mechanics):** C-1 the helper names and shapes of §4.4-4.6. C-2 D-3's bound (§6.2). C-3 whether a kept entry carries its proposal record (§4.7). C-4 sharing the observer list between `admit` and `admit_view` (§6.1). C-5 amending design 06 (§4.15).

## 6. The two small notes

1. **`admit_view` repeats `admit`'s observer list** (`lib.rs:1140-1149`, `deputy.rs:1508-1514`; BACKLOG `:614-618`). Small: one private function holding `admit`'s match, called from both. `admit` returns the same value for every input. Proposed IN.
2. **D-3** (BACKLOG `:629-633`). Smallest sound bound I find: at most ONE kept pair per grant HOLDER, at most 4 holders per session, first come; later pairs counted. One pair proves a holder told two stories, so more from the same holder add nothing, and one deputy can no longer crowd out the evidence about another. Where: `SessionChain::keep_evidence` (`deputy.rs:1214-1226`) with a private holder list beside the pairs. **Not small:** the existing guard of `evidence_unbounded` forks ONE deputy past the bound (two red rows, `scripts/red-controls-roster.sh:131`, `:142`) and the property has two possible holders, so both guards and the generator would need rebuilding. Proposed OUT of this build.

## 7. Attack list for the independent check

1. A starter version with a grant whose epoch is any value but its own number, at a member in mid-session, at a joiner's first list, and at `start_provisional`: each refused.
2. Every starter change kind while admissions stand (nothing, role, invite, remove, limit up and down, grant moved, changed, withdrawn, keep some, keep all, drop all, an intake approval): afterwards no provisional member at any member, and equal view hashes at an old member, a joiner of that version, and a kept person.
3. An admission in flight across a starter version, in both arrival orders; the old epoch's admissions replayed after it; a new admission with `seq` 0 whose `prev` is the old head.
4. A dropped key offered again by the same deputy, a later deputy, and after a withdraw and re-grant: refused. The starter can still invite it.
5. Can the deputy, a guest or the relay make `settle` or `approve_settling` fail for the starter, or cost a proposer a quota unit through the starter's own keep choice? Is "drop all and withdraw" always accepted?
6. The kept entry: seat, role (never above the proposed one), keys, `added_in`; a kept key that is also on `removed`; two approvers for one person.
7. Evidence made from two HONEST admissions of two epochs: must be `NotOneNumber`/`WrongEpoch`, never proof.
8. Every roster with no grant: validation, hash, encoding, `next_with_cap`, `approve` byte for byte as at `32beb56`.
9. Mutants: each new line of the validator and of `settle` removed or inverted, against the new tests alone.
