## For the owner, in plain words
1. The fix works. When you change the member list, the people your deputy let in are now settled in that same change: kept ones become ordinary members, dropped ones stay out. Every member's computer enforces it, so after any change of yours everyone sees the same group again and newcomers can join. I tested this myself, not only with the builder's tests.
2. Nothing changed for sessions without a deputy: I ran 4,000 random sessions through the old and the new code and the results were identical to the byte.
3. "Dropping is lasting" holds: someone you drop cannot be let back in by the deputy, by a new deputy, or after you take the role away and give it back. Only you can add them.
4. You are never stuck: "drop everyone and end the deputy's role" always works, even when every limit is full.
5. Two small things to tidy (not safety): approving a newcomer with "keep everyone" can, in two rare cases, throw away that newcomer's request where "drop" would have let it through; and one help text in the code still points to an older, two-step way that would drop everyone. Neither affects who is a member.
