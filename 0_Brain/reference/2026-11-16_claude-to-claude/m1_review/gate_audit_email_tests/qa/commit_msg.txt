Mask email addresses in audit previews and summaries (the owner's ruling)

The default redactor gains one rule, patterns::emails: an ASCII address with
a dotted domain is replaced whole by the crate's one mask. A scheme:// token
stays with the URL rule and an scp or ssh target is not masked (the
coordinator's ruling on the grammar). Every other rule, apply_mask, truncate
and preview are unchanged byte for byte; find gains added lines only.

Three red controls (redact_no_email, email_keeps_domain,
email_eats_scp_and_urls), five red rows, tests in tests/ae_email.rs. The
docs and BACKLOG record the misses, the peer-preview limit and the
window-end comment.
