KS-1384 R1 round 2: a stalled re-anchor never pays twice; both anchoring calls bounded

What to know.POST /api/documents/{id}/anchor re-checks its claim immediately before it posts: still its own token and its 10-minute lease still live, renewed in the same UPDATE. If not, it posts nothing and answers 409.
The in-flight read is bounded at 5 s (503, Retry-After 60, nothing posted) and the post at 10 s. A timed-out post keeps the claim, because its outcome is unknown: 503 with Retry-After 600, and a retry is refused until the lease ends. Since round 3 the unknown outcome is also recorded, so a retry after the lease is refused on any anchoring row (QA gate 61, G61-F1).
Deploy hold: R1 deploys only with F0, F-b and both parts of R2, after the census, box by box.
Not measured here.A real anchoring service, real stall lengths, the four platform suites, a running stack, Node 24 (LTS), any chain or box.
