Five admin-labelled routes require a platform administrator; a KYC reviewer may not review their own verification — KS-1845

What to know. These routes now answer 403 to any caller whose role is not SYSTEM_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN (the shared isPlatformRole, which folds ASCII case):
  • POST /api/kyc/:id/review;
  • POST /api/governance/process-expired;
  • POST /api/delegations/admin/process-expired;
  • GET /api/stake/all;
  • GET /api/wallets/sessions.
OWNER, ORG_ADMIN, ISSUER_ADMIN, VERIFIER and ADMIN are refused. The review also refuses the verification’s own subject, with both ids case-folded, and refuses an id that is missing or not a string. It checks the role before the body or the id. A test or tool that called these routes with another role now gets 403.
Red at develop: 18 of 24 (kyc) and 9 of 12 (each of the other four). With the change every new cell passes. 59 of 59 mutants killed, plus 8 of 8 on the id fold. Three existing fixtures changed their test principal only.
