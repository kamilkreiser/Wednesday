Six api-gateway log-line describers can no longer take a request down — KS-1771

What to know. Six log lines in services/api-gateway described a caught value, and wrote the log line, inside the catch that answers the request, with no try of their own: audit-export.ts fail500 and its 502 catch, batch.ts fail500, notifications.ts fail500, the audit-log catch in platform.ts and the document-types catch in verification.ts. A thrown value that cannot be described (a throwing message getter, a Proxy whose getPrototypeOf or ownKeys trap throws, a revoked Proxy) or a logger that throws escaped the catch.
A behaviour change on a request path. Each site now describes the value in its own try (fallback thrown value that could not be described) and logs in another, so its constant answer is always sent. At base, four sites gave no answer at all (the 20 unhandled rejections), the audit-export 502 catch answered 500 from its outer catch, and the notifications handlers handed the throw to the gateway’s errorHandler.
Behaviour for every value that could be described before is unchanged. Each guard is pinned by its own cells: describe guard removed, the four describe arms fail at all six sites; log guard removed, the logger arm fails at all six (12 mutants).
Not measured here. What an unhandled rejection does to a running gateway (the gateway survives them by design, KS 546); the five matching lines in originate (KS 1772); and the authentication posture of the document-lookup route (KS 1773). The four platform suites are UNMEASURED and not claimed as a pass.
Node v26.8.1, not the repository’s stated LTS line of Node 24 (no Node 24 is installed on this host). The figures are declared as measured on v26.8.1, not claimed as LTS.
