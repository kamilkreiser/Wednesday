KS-1384 F0c: thread-mint behind a 30 s wallet-lock wait, coins from the wallet view

What to know.POST /api/anchors/thread-mint answers 503 before signing when the wallet is busy for 30 s, when no coin is spendable, or when the caller has gone; it never queues behind an anchor’s confirmation poll.
Its seed and inputs come from the wallet view, and it is recorded there from signing, so anchors and mints never pick the same coin in one process.
Deploy hold: no F0 build where STATE_THREAD_NFT_ENABLED=true without this change; F0 deploys with F-b and R after the census.
