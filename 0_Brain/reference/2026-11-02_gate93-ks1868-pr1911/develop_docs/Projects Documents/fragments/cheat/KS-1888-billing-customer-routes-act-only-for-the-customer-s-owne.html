Billing customer routes act only for the customer’s owner or a platform administrator — KS-1888

What to know. Twelve billing routes that take a customer, invoice or payment-method id, plus POST /customers, now act only for the customer’s owner (metadata.userId equals the caller’s userId, and the tenant where one is recorded) or a platform administrator. Anyone else gets the same 404 as an unknown id. PUT /customers/:id refuses the certificationCredits, userId and tenantId metadata keys with 400. GET /customers/by-email now runs at all.
A test or tool that calls these routes needs a caller who owns the customer, or a platform administrator. Its Stripe double needs findCustomer (and getPaymentMethod or listCustomersByEmail where the route uses them).
A customer created before this change carries whatever userId was last written to it before this change, by its creator or by any signed-in caller through the old PUT /customers/:id, and the check trusts it. The audit of existing customers is KS 1909. That is a limit of the fix, not a finding: there is no other owner record to check against.
Not measured here. A running stack; Stripe; callers outside this repository; how many existing customers carry a wrong userId; the four platform suites; Node 24; any deploy.
