. A refused anchor is retried only when no attempt of the same payload can still land, and an anchor with nothing to spend waits outside the wallet lock (KS-1384 F0b)

What this changes.The attempt family (services/anchoring/src/attemptFamily.ts, new). Every transaction this process signs for one anchor id and one payload (compared as the ENCODED label JSON) is a member of one family. The first member’s largest pass-1 input is the family outpoint, ties broken by transaction hash, then index. Every later member spends that outpoint too, so at most one member can ever reach the ledger.
The boot arm. The family is process-local, so it cannot see a transaction signed before the process started. Until an answered live tip shows a slot at or past S_boot + ANCHOR_TTL_SLOTS + 600, the old rule stands: a reply that says “already been included” rests the row. S_boot is the first answered tip with a positive slot, and the arm reads the builder’s own ANCHOR_TTL_SLOTS. The precondition the code cannot enforce, written in the module header: the old container is stopped before the new one’s first tip read (recreate, no overlap), with one anchoring process per wallet.
Guard 3 and the retry (anchorSubmission.ts). Once armed, a refused member is retried only after EVERY member of its family re-polls absent; a member that may still land is never built past. A1: a spender this process cannot see that carries the same payload confirms the row. A row naming a transaction this process did not sign rests. Rule 4: a family outpoint held by our own live member waits; one held by our own confirmed non-member closes the family; a different payload’s spender closes it only at 3 blocks deep; an unknown spender rests. The retry’s failed write expects (submitting, the refused hash) and counts: 1 schedules the retry, 0 means a newer call owns the row, and null means no bump and no retry. The in-lock confirmed write expects submitting or submitted with its own hash, and names it.
Waits outside the lock. With nothing spendable, or a family outpoint held by our own in-flight member, the anchor re-queues with the wallet lock free and is never failed inside the bound. One marker is written at the first wait, t0 is kept in memory per anchor, and at t0 + 2 h the anchor fails, guarded, once. A write-ahead that lands clears t0.
Gate 48’s fix (round 2, F1). A family whose first member was built only from add-backs has no family outpoint, so nothing would stop a second member being built on other coins. Now, while such a family has a member that may still land, a new attempt builds nothing and waits outside the lock, like rule 4; once that member confirms the row is confirmed from it, and once it is dead the next attempt builds. Cells (nf1)-(nf3) in ks1384-f0b-r2-no-shared-coin-waits.test.ts: (nf1) is red against the round-1 code.
What it means for money (the card’s words). This executes Kam’s 2026-09-14 retry ruling under his 2026-09-25 absence rule, and drops the KS-705 “already included” text carve-out once armed. F0 means MORE transactions land in today’s one-anchor-per-document model: a refused event is retried and lands about 27 minutes later, one more fee per refused event. Anchors may wait up to 2 h when the wallet has nothing to spend. For about 2 h after each anchoring restart F0 keeps today’s behaviour.
