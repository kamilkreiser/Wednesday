. The platform templates and clone-to-tenant 500 answers stop putting the thrown text in details.detail (KS-1410)

What this changes. Blockchain/Dev/services/api-gateway/src/routes/platform.ts (+5/-2, two catch blocks). A response-shape change: the 500 answer of the templates read (message: Failed to fetch templates) and of POST /api/platform/templates/clone-to-tenant (message: Clone failed) carried the thrown text in error.details.detail. The details key is gone from both. The templates catch now logs the thrown text with console.error('[Templates] Failed to fetch templates:', err?.message); the clone catch already logged [Clone] Failed: and only drops details. platform.ts is not one of the six files KS-1410 names: a census of .status(500).json( found these two sites.
New test ks1410-platform-templates-500-never-echoes-the-thrown-text.test.ts, 5 cells, a source-text guard (createPlatformRoutes needs two pools and a wallet): P1 and P2 are RED (no 500 answer in the file names a caught value or carries details; the templates catch logs the thrown text just before it answers); C1 to C3 are controls (the scanner flags a details leak and passes the constant bodies; the file keeps its four 500 answers with their own messages; the clone catch still logs the thrown text).
