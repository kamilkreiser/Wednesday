60.3 What the cells prove, and what they do not

Source wiring, not browser behaviour. Cells 5, 6 and 7 pin that the guard statements exist and stand in the right ORDER — take a sequence number, await, compare, then write state; and for Generate, check and raise before the await and lower after it. They cannot show that a stale answer is really dropped in a browser, or that a second click really mints nothing. frontend/admin has no unit-test tier (KS-1391), so a source guard is what is available; the behaviour is covered by the Playwright cells in tests/e2e/tests/sysadmin-exhaustive.admin.spec.ts, which need the local stack and are item C of the ticket.
