96. Cell 15.2.5 (Process DSR) reaches its PATCH, completes the request and reads it back; 15.2.5b pins the 400 for “processed” (KS-1113)

What this changes. KS-1113, test-only: in gdpr-compliance.prelaunch.spec.ts cell 15.2.5 now creates its DSR with the route’s own body (type, userId from GET /api/auth/me, email, notes), reads the id from request.id and fails when it is missing, PATCHes status: 'completed' with processedBy, asserts 200, success: true and DSR updated, then reads the request back and asserts status is completed with processedBy and completedAt set. The PUT fallback is gone: the route allows GET and PATCH only. A new cell, 15.2.5b, sends status: 'processed' and expects 400 VALIDATION_ERROR. The spec goes from 17 to 18 cells.
Why this is wider than the ticket’s PATCH-body fix: at develop the cell never reached its PATCH. The create body it sent (subjectEmail, reason) is refused with 400 (“type, userId, and email are required”), and had it been accepted the cell would have passed without testing anything, because the new request’s id is at request.id and the cell read id, requestId and data.id and then returned.
