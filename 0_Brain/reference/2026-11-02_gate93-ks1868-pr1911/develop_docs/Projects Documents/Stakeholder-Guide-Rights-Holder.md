## 1. Executive Summary

You are the reason Secuura exists. Whether you are a graduate with a certified degree, an employee with a verified reference, or a professional with certified qualifications — your certified documents are valuable assets that prove your achievements and credentials.

As a Rights Holder or Subject, you have full control over who can access and verify your certifications. You can share them with employers, banks, regulators, or anyone who needs to confirm your credentials — instantly, securely, and with blockchain-backed proof.

**What you can do:**
- View all certifications made about you
- Share certifications with anyone (employers, banks, regulators)
- Control who has access to your certifications
- Transfer ownership rights to others
- Export your data at any time
- Request deletion of your personal data

## 2. Understanding Your Role

### Subject vs Rights Holder

| Role | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Subject** | The person or entity the document is about | A graduate named on a degree certificate |
| **Rights Holder** | The person or entity who controls the certification | Initially the subject; can be transferred |

In most cases, you are both the Subject and the Rights Holder. For example, when a university certifies your degree, you are the subject of the degree and you hold the rights to control who can verify it.

### What You Control

| Action | Your Permission |
|--------|----------------|
| View certification details | Always |
| Share with third parties | Always (as Rights Holder) |
| Transfer rights to another person | Always (as Rights Holder) |
| Request verification | Always |
| Request revocation | Via the certifier |
| Delete your personal data | Via GDPR data subject request |

### What You Cannot Do

| Action | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| Modify the certification | Certifications are immutable — only the original certifier can issue changes |
| Revoke the certification | Only the certifier can revoke |
| Delete the blockchain record | Blockchain records are permanent by design (but personal data can be removed) |

## 3. Receiving a Certification

### How You Are Notified

When a document about you is certified on Secuura:

1. **Email notification** — You receive an email with:
   - The document title
   - The certifying organisation
   - A unique Document ID
   - A verification link

2. **In-app notification** — If you have a Secuura account, a notification appears in your dashboard

### What the Notification Contains

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Document Title** | Name of the certified document |
| **Certifier** | Organisation or individual who certified it |
| **Certification Date** | When the certification was created |
| **Document ID** | Unique identifier for the certification |
| **Verification Link** | Direct link to verify the certification |
| **Blockchain Reference** | Transaction hash on Cardano |

## 4. Viewing Your Certifications

### Via the Verification Portal (No Account Needed)

1. Navigate to the Secuura Verification Portal
2. Enter your Document ID or click the verification link from your notification
3. View the full certification details

### Via Your Secuura Account

If you have a Secuura account:

1. Log in to the portal
2. Navigate to "My Certifications"
3. View all certifications where you are the Subject or Rights Holder
4. Click any certification to see full details, including:
   - Document metadata
   - Certification timeline
   - Blockchain transaction details
   - Verification history
   - Document lineage (visual history)

## 5. Sharing Certifications

### Why Share?

When you apply for a job, a loan, a professional licence, or any situation where someone needs to confirm your credentials, you can share your Secuura certification. The recipient can instantly verify the document without contacting the original issuer.

### How to Share

**Method 1: Share Link**

1. Open the certification you want to share
2. Click "Share"
3. Copy the verification link
4. Send the link to the verifier via email, messaging, or any channel

The recipient clicks the link and sees the verification result instantly.

**Method 2: Email Share**

1. Open the certification
2. Click "Share"
3. Enter the recipient's email address
4. Optional: Add a personal message
5. Click "Send"

The recipient receives an email with the verification link and your message.

**Method 3: QR Code**

1. Open the certification
2. Generate a QR code
3. Add the QR code to your CV, portfolio, or business card
4. Anyone scanning the QR code can verify the certification instantly

### Who Can See Your Certifications?

By default, certifications are private. Only people you share with (via link, email, or QR code) can access the verification details. The blockchain record is public (as are all blockchain records), but it contains only a cryptographic hash — not your personal information.

## 6. Transferring Rights

### What is Rights Transfer?

Rights transfer allows you to pass ownership control of a certification to another person or entity. This is relevant when:

- You are selling a property and need to transfer the title certification
- An employer acquires rights to employee certifications
- You are transferring a certification-linked asset as part of a corporate restructuring

### How to Transfer Rights

1. Open the certification
2. Click "Transfer Rights"
3. Enter the recipient's wallet address
4. Choose the transfer type:

| Transfer Type | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Simple** | Direct owner-to-owner transfer of document rights |
| **Sale** | Transfer linked to a verified payment transaction (requires both parties' approval) |
| **Inheritance** | Transfer of rights following probate/legal documentation (requires executor approval) |
| **Corporate** | Transfer requiring corporate governance approval (e.g., board resolution) |

5. Provide reason and any required documentation references
6. The transfer enters `pending_approval` status
7. The recipient is notified and must approve the transfer
8. Once approved, the transfer is recorded on blockchain

### Important Notes

- **Transfers are permissionless** — You do not need the certifier's approval to transfer your rights
- **Transfers are recorded on blockchain** — The transfer history is part of the document's provenance chain
- **Full transfers are permanent** — Once transferred, you no longer have control over the certification
- **Limited transfers** can have conditions and expiration dates

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Secuura is fully GDPR-compliant. You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### Right of Access (GDPR Article 15)

You can request a complete export of all personal data Secuura holds about you. This includes:
- Your profile information
- All certifications (as subject or rights holder)
- Consent records
- Verification history
- Share records

### Right to Erasure (GDPR Article 17)

You can request deletion of your personal data. When processed:
- Your user profile is anonymised
- Your personal metadata is removed
- Consent records are deleted
- Share records are deleted

**Note:** Blockchain records (cryptographic hashes only) cannot be deleted as they contain no personal data. The hash becomes an orphaned reference with no link to your identity.

### Right to Data Portability (GDPR Article 20)

You can download all your data in a structured, machine-readable JSON format.

### How to Exercise Your Rights

1. Contact Secuura at privacy@secuura.io
2. Or submit a Data Subject Request through your account settings
3. Secuura will process your request within 30 days (as required by GDPR)

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a Secuura account to receive a certification?**  
A: No. You will receive an email notification with a Document ID and verification link. An account gives you additional features (dashboard, sharing controls, transfer capabilities).

**Q: Can I share my certification with multiple people?**  
A: Yes. You can share via link, email, or QR code with as many people as you wish.

**Q: What if I lose the verification link?**  
A: If you have a Secuura account, you can access all your certifications from your dashboard. If not, contact the issuing organisation or Secuura support with your details.

**Q: Can anyone see my certifications without my permission?**  
A: No. Certifications are private by default. Only people you share with can view the full details. The blockchain contains only a cryptographic hash — not your name, document content, or any personal information.

**Q: What if I disagree with a certification?**  
A: Contact the certifying organisation directly. Only the certifier can revoke or amend a certification.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**  
A: A mobile application is planned for a future release. Currently, the platform is accessible via web browser on any device.

**Q: What happens to my certifications if Secuura ceases operations?**  
A: Your blockchain-anchored certifications are permanent and publicly verifiable on the Cardano blockchain, independent of Secuura's operations. The hashes can be verified using any Cardano explorer.
