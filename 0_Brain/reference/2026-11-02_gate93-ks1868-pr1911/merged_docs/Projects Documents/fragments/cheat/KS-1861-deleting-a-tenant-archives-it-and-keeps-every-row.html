Deleting a tenant archives it and keeps every row — KS-1861

What to know. DELETE /api/platform/tenants/:id with {"confirm": true} now sets the tenant’s status to archived and answers { success: true, deleted: false, archived: true, status: "archived", message }. No row is deleted and no database is dropped, whatever PROVISION_PER_TENANT_DB says. Erasure is not built (Kam: “Archive, not erase”).
An already-archived tenant answers 409. A status change made by someone else between the read and the update answers 409 too. A failed archive answers the constant 500 and leaves the tenant as it was. The audit log shows tenant_archived where it used to show tenant_deleted; a failed archive is recorded as tenant_archive_failed on a best-effort basis. To reverse an archive, move the tenant to suspended with PATCH /api/platform/tenants/:id/status, then to active; archived → active in one step answers 409 (Kam, card secuura-ks1861-archive-from-which-status-1101: “b — As (a), and archived can go back to suspended”).
Status reads at start-up stay as built (Kam, 2026-11-01 10:05:24 AEDT, card secuura-ks1860-status-refresh-fails-at-startup-1101: “a — Keep it as built and say so”). Where MULTI_TENANCY_ENABLED is on: if the platform database is down when originate starts, originate serves every tenant, suspended and archived ones included, until a status read succeeds. A tenant suspended after the last good read is served for as long as the database stays down. This is not a regression: before KS 1860 nothing refused a suspended tenant at all.
Red at develop: 9 of 12, and 1 of 18 for the status order (archived → suspended). Green after: 12 of 12 and 18 of 18. Mutants: 16 of 16 killed.
