KS-1384 F-b: the reconciler settles anchor rows that have no transaction hash

What to know.A pending anchor row with no hash, which no running process is driving and nobody has written for 10 minutes, is now searched for on chain: confirmed if its payload is there, failed (“nothing was submitted”) only after a complete search finds nothing.
It never sweeps a row this process still drives, a row inside its 2 h wait (+15 min), or anything inside the boot arm’s window (about 2 h 10 min) after a restart; and a re-POST during the search wins.
Before giving up a row with a hash, the reconciler searches for its payload under another hash and adopts it. Placeholder hashes (mock_tx_, tx_sim_) are still never touched (KS-1870).
