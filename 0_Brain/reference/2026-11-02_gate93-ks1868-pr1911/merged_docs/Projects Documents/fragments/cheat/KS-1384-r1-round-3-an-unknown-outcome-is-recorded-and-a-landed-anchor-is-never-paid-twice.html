KS-1384 R1 round 3: a re-anchor whose outcome is unknown is recorded; a landed anchor is never paid for twice

What to know.When POST /api/documents/{id}/anchor cannot tell whether its post landed (no answer in 10 s, or a 2xx whose body is lost), the blob records retryPostUnknownAt and the claim is kept: 503, Retry-After 600. After that, any anchoring row for the document (confirmed or failed) means 409, never a post; only “no row” read after the lease lets it post. Anchoring writes its row before it spends the fee, which is what makes that safe.
A 2xx gets 10 s more for its body after its headers arrive, so a slow 202 now succeeds. The re-check before the post also refuses a revoked or deleted document and a confirmed blob. The whole budget is 25 s against the 10-minute lease.
A marked document stays refused on a failed row until R2b’s resolver decides it. Deploy hold: R1 deploys only with F0, F-b, R2a and R2b, and with G58-F3’s fix (R2a: anchoring’s by-document read answers 503 on a database fault, not the absent 404), after the census, box by box. QA gate 65 (GO WITH NOTES) added G58-F3 to the hold, because the marker’s clear trusts that 404 as “no row”.
Not measured here.A real anchoring service, real post latencies and stall lengths, the four platform suites, a running stack, Node 24 (LTS), any chain or box.
