Owner-less credentials are read and revoked by platform administrators of their tenant — KS-1906

What to know. A vc-issuer credential with no recorded owner (created_by_user_id NULL) is now read and revoked only by a platform administrator (isPlatformRole: SYSTEM_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN) whose tenant is the row’s tenant (Kam’s ruling on card secuura-ks1851-ownerless-credentials-who-1031, extending his KS 1695 rule). For those rows an ORG_ADMIN is refused and a SUPER_ADMIN admitted. A connector is refused: KS 1695’s rule admits only the connector that created a record, and an owner-less credential has no creation record. Whether connectors should read them anyway is a card for Kam (secuura-ks1906-ownerless-credentials-connectors-1101). A row with no recorded tenant is readable by nobody. Owned rows and presentations are unchanged.
