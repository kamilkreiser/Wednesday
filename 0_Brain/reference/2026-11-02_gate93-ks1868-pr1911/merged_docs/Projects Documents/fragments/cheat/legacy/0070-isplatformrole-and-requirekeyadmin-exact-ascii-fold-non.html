isPlatformRole and requireKeyAdmin: exact ASCII fold, non-string role refused — KS-1591

What to know. The platform-administrator predicate (isPlatformRole, packages/shared) and the key-admin gate in front of the key routes (requireKeyAdmin, services/security) upper-cased the role with the locale-aware toUpperCase() after stringifying it. A role with U+0131 or U+017F in it, or an array holding an admin role, therefore read as an admin role. Both now apply an ASCII-only fold and refuse a role that is not a string.
Every spelling admitted before (SYSTEM_ADMIN, SUPER_ADMIN, ISSUER_ADMIN and their lower and mixed case forms) is admitted now; the change can only remove access. Two local-model passes, applied unchanged.
Not measured here. Whether such a token can reach either predicate in production, the other importers of isPlatformRole (their suites were not run), and a Node 24 LTS run (the host is Node 26.8.1).
A running stack and the four platform suites (Schemathesis, Akto, Playwright, Performance/k6) are not claimed as passing. The change is not deployed.
