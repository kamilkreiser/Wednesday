. The document anchor retry route: a claimant stalled past its lease never pays alongside a newer retry, and both anchoring calls are bounded (KS-1384 R1 round 2)

What this changes.The race it closes (QA gate 58, G58-F1). R1’s claim on POST /api/documents/{id}/anchor is a 10-minute lease (REANCHOR_CLAIM_TTL_MS), so a crashed holder cannot wedge the document. But the holder had no deadline and never looked at its claim again before it posted. A holder stalled past the lease (a hung read of anchoring, a frozen process) could be overtaken by a newer retry, which found no row and posted; then the old holder posted too. Two fees for one document (gate 58’s cell Q2a-stale).
The re-check, immediately before the post (documentRepo.ts, new confirmReanchorClaim). One conditional UPDATE: it matches only while the blob still carries this call’s token AND its lease is still live, and it renews the lease. 0 rows means a newer retry holds the document, or this call’s own lease lapsed: nothing is posted, the claim is given back (guarded on its own token) and the answer is 409. The renewal is what lets the bounded post run under a fresh lease, so no takeover can start while it is in flight.
Both anchoring calls are bounded (services/reanchorInFlight.ts, routes/documents.ts). The in-flight read has 5 s and the post 10 s: 15 s together, 2.5% of the lease. A read that times out is “anchoring cannot be read” (503, Retry-After 60, nothing posted, claim given back). A post that times out has an UNKNOWN outcome, because anchoring writes its row before it answers 202 and spends the fee afterwards; so the claim is KEPT (no release, no outcome write) and the answer is 503 with Retry-After 600. A retry inside those 10 minutes is refused by the claim; after them, the in-flight read sees anchoring’s row, but only while that row is unconfirmed: a row that had meanwhile confirmed read as “nothing in flight”, and that retry paid a second fee (QA gate 61, G61-F1). Round 3, the next entry, records the unknown outcome so that this retry is refused on any row.
Who sees a different answer: a stalled claimant that would have paid a second fee is refused (409); a hung anchoring read or post now ends in 503 after 5 s or 10 s instead of hanging. Nothing else on the route changes.

.3 What this does not prove

Stated, not claimed.A process frozen for longer than the lease between the renewal and the post leaving the socket is not covered; real stall lengths are not measured (gate 58 forced the stall by backdating the claim, as these cells do).
The token is a random value, so the re-check’s tenant condition cannot be shown separately by a cell; no kill is claimed for it.
A real anchoring service, the four platform suites, a running stack, Node 24 (LTS) and any chain or box: not run here. A landed anchor read as “nothing in flight” (G58-F2) and anchoring’s fail-open read (G58-F3) are R2a’s.
