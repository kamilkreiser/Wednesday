. A by-email lookup returns the caller’s own customer when two customers share an email (KS-1911)

What this changes. Tests, and one code comment; no product behaviour changes. KS 1888 bound GET /billing/customers/by-email to the customer’s owner: of the customers Stripe lists for the email, it returns one the caller may act on (billing.ts, listCustomersByEmail(email).find(…mayActOnCustomer…)). QA gate 62, finding F3: no cell pinned the case of two customers on one email. Its mutant M18, which sends the route back to Stripe’s first match (findCustomerByEmail) and checks only that one, survived all 211 cells of the pull request. Such a regression costs availability, not a leak: the owner would get 404 on their own lookup whenever another user’s customer with the same email comes first.
Four cells in ks1888-billing-routes-bound-to-the-customer-owner.test.ts, two customers on dup@a.test with Bob’s first in Stripe’s order:

  • E2, a control: Bob gets his own;
  • E3 (QA gate 69, note N1): a signed-in user of the same tenant who owns neither customer gets 404, byte-identical to an unknown email.
