. Billing’s customer, invoice and payment-method routes admit only the customer’s owner or a platform administrator (KS-1888)

What this changes. Twelve billing routes took a Stripe customer, invoice or payment-method id from the caller and acted on it with no check of who was asking: read or change any customer, spend its certification credits, list or detach its payment methods, read its invoices. POST /billing/customers also returned any existing customer to whoever posted its email, and PUT /billing/customers/:id let a caller write metadata.certificationCredits, which is where the balance lives.
The rule (the coordinator’s D1–D4, 2026-10-31): a caller may act on a customer when they are a platform administrator (the shared isPlatformRole, as KS-1850) or its owner: metadata.userId equals the caller’s userId, both non-empty strings, exact compare, and where the customer records metadata.tenantId it equals the caller’s. An invoice or payment method belongs to the owner of its customer; with no customer, or on a customer with no recorded owner, it is platform administrators only. Tenant roles get nothing beyond their own customer.
A refused caller gets the route’s own 404, byte-identical to an unknown id; no Stripe write or listing runs and nothing is logged. GET /customers/:id/credits now answers 404 for an unknown id too (it read as a zero balance). POST /customers takes the owner and tenant from the token, refuses a body userId that is not the caller’s with 403 (a platform administrator may create for another user), and returns an existing customer only to someone who may act on it; anyone else gets a new customer of their own. PUT refuses certificationCredits, userId and tenantId in metadata with 400, for everyone. GET /customers/by-email moved above GET /customers/:id: below it, Express sent /customers/by-email to the :id handler, so it never ran.
A customer created before this change carries whatever userId was last written to it before this change, by its creator or by any signed-in caller through the old PUT /customers/:id, and the check trusts it. The audit of existing customers is KS 1909. That is a limit of the fix, not a finding: there is no other owner record to check against.

.3 What this does not prove

Unit level only. The Stripe service is a mock in every route cell and the Stripe SDK in every lookup cell; no real or test Stripe account was called. How many existing customers carry a userId that is not their real owner’s is not measured. No in-repo caller of these routes exists (the one frontend component is not imported anywhere), so callers outside this repository are not measured.
Not run: a running stack, the four platform suites (Schemathesis, Akto, Playwright, Performance/k6), Node 24, any deploy. The checkout routes’ req.user.stripeCustomerId, which nothing sets, is a separate ticket.
