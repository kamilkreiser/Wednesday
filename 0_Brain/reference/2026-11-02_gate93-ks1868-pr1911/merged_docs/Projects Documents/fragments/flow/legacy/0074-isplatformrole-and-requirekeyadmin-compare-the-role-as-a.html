85. isPlatformRole and requireKeyAdmin compare the role as an exact ASCII fold and refuse a role that is not a string (KS-1591)

What this changes. KS-1591 (a security nit, pre-existing): two predicates decided “platform administrator” and “key administrator” with String(role).toUpperCase(), which is not an identity test. U+0131 (dotless i) upper-cases to I, U+017F (long s) upper-cases to S, and a role ARRAY stringifies to its element, so a role spelled with either character, or an array holding SUPER_ADMIN, read as an admin role. isPlatformRole in packages/shared/src/security/keyRevokePolicy.ts and requireKeyAdmin in services/security/src/index.ts now apply an ASCII fold only (a-z to A-Z, every other character untouched) and treat a role that is not a string as no role. Every ASCII spelling admitted before is admitted now. Two local-model passes (the Spark, DeepSeek V4 Flash: ks_1591_p1 and ks_1591_p2_security) each passed their checker 7 of 7 and are byte-identical to the drafter’s golden; both were applied here strictly and unchanged.
In each product file one line is replaced by four (+4 / −1). Two test files are added: packages/shared/src/__tests__/ks1591-is-platform-role-is-an-exact-ascii-fold.test.ts (vitest, 13 cells) and services/security/src/__tests__/ks1591-require-key-admin-is-an-exact-ascii-fold.test.ts (vitest, 7 cells; it boots the real app over HTTP with an RS256 token). The change fails toward LESS access. No tier timing or budget in these documents moves.

85.2 What the cells prove, and what they do not

The two predicates, not their callers. The shared cells pin that U+0131, U+017F and a role array are refused by isPlatformRole, that every ASCII spelling the platform uses is still admitted, that an ordinary, an empty and an absent role are still refused, and (D1, D2) that decideTenantAccess, the caller, sees the refusal.
