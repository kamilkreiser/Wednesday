107. A certification whose anchor was refused is kept and says so: a top-level anchoringStatus on the issue and recertify responses (KS-1709)

What this changes. Kam’s ruling b on KS-1709 (2026-10-23): “Keep it, but mark it unanchored”. Outside production, POST /api/certifications/issue and POST /api/certifications/:id/recertify already kept a certification whose anchor was refused and stored blockchain.anchoringStatus failed with status failed (KS 1124 F4). The 201 showed that only inside blockchainAnchor (issue) or blockchain (recertify), beside confidence pending-onchain. Both 201 bodies now also carry a top-level anchoringStatus of failed.
The field is present only when the anchoring service did not accept the request in this call: a 4xx, a 5xx, an unreachable service, or the issue route’s 10 second timeout. It does not mean no anchor exists: after a timeout or a thrown error one may. When the anchor is accepted the key is absent, and the response and the stored blob are unchanged (cells I4 and Q4 pin the full key set).
Production is unchanged. A refused anchor still answers 503 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE with the same body: the issue route removes its provisional row, the recertify route saves nothing. Cells I5, I6, Q5 and Q6 pin the body and the saves.
No migration: the anchor state rides in documents.certification_metadata (blockchain), and the documents table has no anchoring status column. Contract: a new schema CertificationIssueResponse (Certification plus an optional anchoringStatus, enum failed) is the 201 of the issue route only. Certification is unchanged, the blockchain block stays untyped (KS 1019), and the recertify route is not in the published contract and is not added. The yaml changes by 22 lines added and 1 removed, 313 paths, sha16 6617e52eaa6093e0 to 6de976a5cb39a903.
