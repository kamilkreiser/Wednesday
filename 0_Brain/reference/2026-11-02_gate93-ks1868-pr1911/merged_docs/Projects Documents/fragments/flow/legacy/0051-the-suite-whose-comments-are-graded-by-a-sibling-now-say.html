62.1 Why the warning quotes none of the three strings

The obvious draft breaks the thing it documents. A warning that helpfully quoted the required sentence would add a second occurrence of it to the graded file, and CELL 2 counts occurrences across the whole file, not within a block. So the useful-looking version of this notice reds the cell it exists to protect. The committed notice therefore describes each string by its role and its required count, and says in-line why it does not quote them.
This was driven, not reasoned about: the patch script asserts the three counts are (0, 1, 1) after its own edit and refuses to write the file if its warning moved any of them. A deliberately quoting draft was then graded as the red arm below.
