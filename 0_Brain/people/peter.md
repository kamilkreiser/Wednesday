# Peter — Secuura reviewer

**Role:** reviews and personally tests Secuura/Blockchain work. Kam's framing (2026-08-06): Peter reads
*every* ticket himself and it takes him a long time — which is why the BLUF rule exists
([[../learnings/2026-08-06_bluf-write-for-the-reader]]) and why handovers to him are TEST BLOCKS rather
than lists of PRs ([[../learnings/2026-09-05_handovers-to-peter-and-stuart-are-test-blocks]]).

**TIMEZONE — United Kingdom (Kam, 2026-09-07 09:53).** AEST runs ~9-10 hours ahead. **Anything sent in
Kam's morning does not reach Peter until late in the Australian day**, and his replies land in
Wednesday's evening or overnight.

**How to apply:**
1. **Never read Peter's silence during the AEST working day as a non-response** — it is the middle of
   his night. A "quiet since X" claim about Peter carries the timezone or it is meaningless
   ([[../learnings/2026-08-15_a-cap-is-never-neutral]] — a window that opens at the wrong instant).
2. **Expect his review latency to be a day, not hours**, and size the queue put to him accordingly.
   This is part of why the board's create-vs-close ratio looks the way it does; it is structural, not
   neglect.
3. **The overnight fleet window is Peter's working day.** Work landing for him at 23:00 AEST arrives at
   the start of his morning — which makes the overnight grant
   ([[../learnings/2026-08-28_overnight-is-working-time]]) more valuable than it first appears.
4. Kam sends anything client-facing; nobody else messages Peter
   ([[../learnings/2026-09-05_tickets-are-the-channel-whatsapp-via-kam-is-the-escalation]]).
