# In voice interaction: one question, one answer

**The lesson:** Batches of five-plus questions work for coding sessions, but for
voice interaction ask ONE question per turn and wait for the answer. Kam finds it
hard to keep many balls in the air and answer carefully when several questions
arrive at once.

**Context:** During discovery I ended turns with four questions at a time. Kam
explicitly corrected this: careful, diligent answers need single questions.

**How to apply:**
1. Any turn expecting a spoken (Whisperflow) reply carries at most one question.
2. Keep a private queue (discovery doc, TASKS.md) and surface the next question
   only after the current one is answered.
3. It's fine to say "three more in the queue" so Kam knows what's coming — just
   don't ask them yet.
4. Dense multi-part *statements* are fine; it's parallel *questions* that overload.
