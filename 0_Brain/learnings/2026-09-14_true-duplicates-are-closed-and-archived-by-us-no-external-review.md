# True duplicates are closed and archived by US — no external review, no comment asking for one. Standing rule, all boards.

**His words, verbatim (2026-09-14 08:55 AEST):**
> *"On second thought if these are truly duplicates, no need for external review or comment, let's
> just close them and archive them ourselves. This should be a standing rule going forward."*

**The operative case, so the headline matches it:** a board pass (a census, a dedupe sweep, a
class-h pass, a triage) has established at SOURCE that ticket B asks for the same defect or the same
work as ticket A. **Wednesday's seat marks B `Duplicate of A`, closes it and archives it — with ONE
facts-only comment on B naming A and the read that proved it — and nobody outside is asked.** The
survivor A keeps every relation and comment that B carried nothing unique of. No card, no proposal
comment, no "Peter rules per cluster".

**What it does NOT cover, stated so the rule is not read wide:**
1. **Overlapping-but-distinct tickets are NOT duplicates.** Shared words, a shared file, a shared
   cause with different asks — those stay open, unmerged, and are not closed under this rule. The
   test is the one s219 applied: *same defect or same ask, established from the tickets' own words
   and their PRs at source* — never from titles alone ([[2026-09-07_a-classification-list-is-a-representation-not-an-instruction]]).
2. **Tickets written by Peter or Stuart** are still theirs to close when they are the DUPLICATE; when
   one of theirs is the SURVIVOR, ours closes into it under this rule. (Kam's 2026-09-06 10:24: "once
   something is assigned to someone it belongs to them.")
3. **A duplicate with something unique** (a comment, a measurement, a relation the survivor lacks)
   is closed only after that thing is carried onto the survivor — the comment names what moved.
4. **Reversible by construction:** archive, never delete; the comment on B is the audit trail.

**How to apply:**
1. **Every board-pass brief carries the standing line:** *"TRUE duplicates (same defect or ask,
   proven at source): mark `Duplicate of <survivor>`, close, archive, ONE facts-only comment — no
   proposal, no external review (Kam 2026-09-14 08:55). Overlapping-but-distinct: untouched."*
   → `2_Project_Files/fleet/STANDING_LINES.md` and the board-seat brief shape (s218/s219).
2. **The survivor rule stays what s219 used:** the ticket with the fixing PR; else the older id; else
   the one a client human wrote. Stated in the comment.
3. **The 2026-09-14 instance is executed by s219 itself** (its ADDENDUM): the five TRUE DUPLICATE
   clusters (C4 → KS-848; C20 → KS-826; C23 → KS-1026; C24 → KS-987; C31 → KS-783; seven relations,
   all Backlog) closed + archived; its KS-485 comment corrected by a follow-up line so Peter is not
   asked to rule on those five; the 25 overlapping rows stand as information.
4. **It generalises to every board Wednesday's seats work** — the rule is his, "going forward", and
   not scoped to Platform K.
