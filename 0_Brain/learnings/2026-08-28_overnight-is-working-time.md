# Overnight is working time — agents run until their queue is finished; Kam reviews in the morning

**The grant (recorded per go-slow rule 5):** when a project has tasks outstanding —
the shape the Secuura relay took on 2026-08-27 (five legs, 19:17 → 23:11, queue dry) —
the agents do NOT stop for the night. They keep going, session after session, until
everything agent-actionable is finished; Kam and I review the result together in
the morning. This generalises the per-night grants of 08-12 (HPSM readiness) and
08-27 19:46 ("keep pushing now or through the night") into the standing rhythm.

**What does NOT change:** the v1.3 signature classes (prod, money, external comms,
irreversible) still pause for Kam — "until everything is finished" means everything
inside the delegated scope; a Kam-gated item stops where it always stopped. Quiet
hours for SPEECH (no voice 23:00–06:00) stand. Nothing merges without an approval.

**Mechanism (a promise is not a mechanism — the 08-07 rule):**
1. The 05:30 shift change no longer orders a wrap: its tap tells an agent with a live
   queue to CONTINUE (checkpoint + handover at its own rhythm-§2 boundary), and to wrap
   only if the queue is dry. The 23:00 close bell likewise stops treating a live agent as
   "unwrapped". → WED ticket filed the same session; until it ships, I override the tap
   text by hand at any shift change I am awake for.
2. The relay depends on a live coordinator to score each wrap and launch the successor.
   WED-132 (the login-parked pane) is the failure that breaks this; its fix is now
   load-bearing for the grant. Honest gap, stated: a wrap at 02:00 with no coordinator
   waits until the coordinator is back — the same gap the HPSM grant recorded.
3. The morning brief LEADS with the overnight result (what Peter/whoever returns to),
   not the mechanics — Kam's "review together in the morning" is the delivery point.
