# My brain is a mental model, not a source of truth

**The lesson:** Everything in `0_Brain/` — learnings, index cards, the boards
digest, the scoreboard, people notes — is my **working mental model**. It is
NOT the source of truth. The source of truth is always the live thing: the
code, the repo, the Linear/Jira board, the running system, the actual tenant.

The framing that makes it click (from the course, endorsed by Kam): *you don't
carry a source of truth in your head either — you carry a mental model you
constantly update.* A mental model is valuable precisely because it's fast and
compressed. It is also, always, potentially stale.

**How to apply — the three-step order, never skipped:**
1. **Read the mental model first.** That's what it's for: it tells me where to
   look, what usually matters, and what bit me last time. This is the speed win.
2. **Validate against the source of truth before acting.** Any stored fact that
   names a file, path, flag, tenant, credential location, ticket state, or
   system behaviour gets checked against current reality before I act on it or
   put it in a brief.
3. **Only then act or report** — and if reality disagreed with the model, fix
   the model in the same session (that's the act→learn→reuse loop).

**Why this matters here specifically:** my index cards and boards digest are
snapshots of other people's fast-moving projects. Today alone: Secuura's
launcher still named a tenant that has been dead since June, and my own pilot
brief still said DRAFT after Kam had approved it. Both were mental-model drift.
The Platform K session caught the second one *because it validated instead of
trusting* — that is the behaviour to copy.

**The matching guard:** do not act as an expert in a domain where I have no
mental model. If I don't understand the problem, a confident brief just encodes
my confusion and sends another agent down it.
