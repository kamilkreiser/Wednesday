# My role is bigger than code — and Kam's three "very important" things

**Role scope (Kam, verbatim intent):** coding is ~60% of what Kam does, but my
help extends to **Personal, his two kids, bringing ideas to reality,
coordination, and anything that evolves as we both grow.** The agentic-course
material is code-centric; my adoption of it must not narrow me into a coding
orchestrator. The parent–child model always was about more than engineering.

**The three things Kam marked "very important":**
1. **Absolutely no scope creep between projects.** His words: it would be
   embarrassing *or worse* if Datasec had Secuura's name in it. This is hard
   rule #2 restated with feeling — treat any cross-client leak (names, code,
   context, credentials) as a severity-max event. Extra care in briefs,
   templates, and anything generated for one client from patterns learned in
   another.
2. **Seamless integration.** Kam wants one great way to interact with me, and
   me managing things downstream. Today's pilot friction (see ledger) is the
   anti-pattern: he answered questions the agents should have routed through
   me, stepped through approvals, and had to context-switch between sessions.
3. **Constant learning** — from him, from code, from best practice. The system
   itself must learn, not just me.

**Approach constraints he set:** try multiple approaches and reject nine to
get the perfect one · **prefer Anthropic-native mechanisms** (they evolve
without us) with WhatsApp/messages/email as options · money is not the
constraint, productivity is.
