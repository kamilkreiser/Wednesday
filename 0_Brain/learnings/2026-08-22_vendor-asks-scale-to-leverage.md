# Vendor asks scale to our leverage — best practice is a menu, not a mandate

**The lesson:** a due-diligence ask must be sized to what the counterparty
will actually answer for a customer of our size. The Attio security pack
(SOC 2 under NDA · residency · sub-processors · audit retention · DPA ·
exit terms) is textbook for an enterprise buyer; from a three-seat
trial-tier customer it is a request that gets silence, and a gate nobody
answers blocks us, not them. Kam's framing: right by policy, not achievable
in some cases — and he praised the best-practice instinct while overruling
the application.

**How to apply:**
1. Before drafting any external vendor ask, size it: what does a customer
   of OUR spend realistically get answered? Prefer what is publicly
   published (DPA pages, trust centres, standard terms) read carefully and
   ACCEPTED KNOWINGLY over bespoke requests that will not be answered.
2. When Kam accepts a vendor's public posture, convert "accepted blind"
   into "accepted informed" at zero vendor friction: read the public pages
   at a browser seat, record what the posture actually IS in the decision
   register, date it, and name any residual (e.g. residency unstated) ONCE
   — then yield.
3. A gate we control (like D6) can be re-defined by the owner's informed
   acceptance; the record must show the decision and its date, never a
   silently vanished gate.
4. Keep proposing best practice — he explicitly valued the instinct, TWICE
   (19:51 and again 19:55: "please continue to challenge or ask for best
   practice. However, this is not always possible, and we can make a
   judgement case by case"). The standing shape: I raise the best-practice
   bar every time; HE holds the case-by-case judgement on whether it is
   achievable; a ruled-down bar is recorded, never silently dropped. The
   correction is about the DELIVERY: scale the mechanism to leverage;
   escalate to the heavy ask only when the relationship's weight justifies
   it (an Enterprise-tier negotiation is the moment for the full pack).
