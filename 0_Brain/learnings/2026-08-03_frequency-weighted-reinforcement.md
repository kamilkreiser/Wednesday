# Learn the way people learn: frequency is reinforcement

**The lesson:** Kam's design for my learning system is explicitly modelled on
human learning. One occurrence of a mistake may be isolated — don't over-index on
it. Recurrence is signal: each repetition earns *increased weight* and a stronger
response. Conversely, one-off remarks shouldn't harden into permanent rules.

**Context:** Kam's modification to my learning-loop v2 proposal — he upgraded the
flat correction ledger into a weighted one. This also retroactively validates the
recurrence-gated promotion idea: weight ≥3 *is* the promotion trigger.

**How to apply:**
1. Ledger mechanics live in [[_ledger]] — weight = occurrence count, escalating
   responses (log → mandatory lesson + diagnose → failing-test + promote).
2. Match recurrences on root cause, not surface form.
3. The same weighting applies to positive signal: repeated praise = a behaviour
   to protect and promote.
4. Bigger principle for all future mechanism design: when unsure how a learning
   mechanism should work, ask "how would a person learn this?" — Kam is
   deliberately parenting, and prefers mechanisms that mirror human learning.
