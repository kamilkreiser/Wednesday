# Every instruction that arrives on the panel gets an immediate RECEIPT — one or two sentences, saying it landed and who has it

**His words, verbatim (2026-09-09 16:07:13 +10:00):**

> *"While we refine the operating model of Tuesday and Wednesday, when you get instructions
> through the chat website, can you confirm that instructions have been received and are being
> acted on?"*

**The operative case:** an instruction arrives in the panel. **Before doing the work, before
routing it, before anything — send one or two sentences saying it arrived and what is happening
to it.** Including, and especially, when what is happening is *"that one is Tuesday's and she
has it"*.

## Why he asked, and it is not politeness

In the half hour before he asked, he sent **four** instructions — the NexusAI marketplace
review, the security-review proofread, its no-emoji addendum, and the two-document split. Every
one was routed to Tuesday within a minute. **But he was told in two BUNDLED messages, one of
them several minutes later.** So for stretches of that half hour he had dictated instructions
into a panel and had no way of knowing whether any of them had landed anywhere.

**That is fine for a report and wrong for a receipt.** He is dictating quickly, often between
other things, and the panel gives him no delivery signal of its own. **Silence after an
instruction is indistinguishable from the instruction being lost** — and the fleet's whole
delegation model runs through that panel.

## The distinction that stops this becoming noise

**A RECEIPT and a REPORT are different messages and only the first is instant.**

| | receipt | report |
|---|---|---|
| answers | *did it arrive, and who has it* | *what came of it* |
| length | one or two sentences | as long as it needs |
| timing | **immediately, before the work** | at the natural boundary |
| batching | **never batched** — one per instruction | **consolidated** — several items in one message |

This matters because [[2026-09-08_the-panel-reads-the-whole-message]] made length a real cost
in seconds of his listening time, and [[2026-09-08_ask-format-action-first]] says to
consolidate his open asks rather than dripping them. **Those rules govern REPORTS. They do not
govern receipts** — a two-sentence receipt is a few seconds of audio, and the thing it prevents
is him wondering whether to repeat himself.

## How to apply

1. **Receipt first, work second.** The acknowledgement goes out before the routing mail, before
   the analysis, before opening a file. It costs seconds and it is the only thing standing
   between him and uncertainty.
2. **Say who has it, not just that it arrived.** *"Received — that one is Tuesday's and it is
   with her verbatim"* is a receipt. *"Received"* alone is not, because the question underneath
   his ask is **where did it go**.
3. **One receipt per instruction, never batched.** Four instructions get four receipts. The
   consolidation rule applies to what comes after.
4. **Include the ones that need nothing from me** — a routed item still gets a receipt, because
   from his seat a routed instruction and a dropped one look identical.
5. **If an instruction is ambiguous, the receipt says so and does not wait for the answer:**
   *"Received. Two readings of 'HPALM' — confirming which before anyone acts."* A receipt that
   states a question is still a receipt.
6. **Scope, in his words: "while we refine the operating model of Tuesday and Wednesday."**
   That is a scope clause ([[2026-09-06_a-scoped-override-carries-its-own-expiry]]) but it has
   **no date and no event** — it ends when he says the model has settled. **Read as standing
   until then; stated to him as that reading.** It gets no `doctor.sh` expiry check, because
   there is nothing to check against — and that is said out loud rather than quietly assumed.

## The wider thing worth keeping

This is the third time this fleet has learned the same shape from a different direction: **the
person waiting cannot see the machinery.** An agent's silence reads as working, finished,
stalled or dead ([[2026-08-09_an-enforcement-you-must-arm-is-not-one]] rule 3) — and the
principal's does too, in reverse. **He built the panel so the conversation had a stable surface
([[2026-08-17_conversation-needs-a-stable-panel]]); a surface with no delivery signal is only
half of that.**
