# Autonomy grant: fleet ship/deploy decisions are mine, escalate by exception

**The grant (Kam, verbatim):** *"happy for you to make these decisions yourself
and only escalate to me if needed."*

**Context that matters for reading its scope:** he said it in the same breath as
correcting a misattribution — having just watched me refuse to relay an approval
whose provenance I could not prove, and having been told I would rather stall his
own deploy than carry an unverifiable authorisation. The grant is a response to
that behaviour, so it is trust extended *because* the boundary held, not an
instruction to loosen it. Recorded explicitly per
[[2026-08-03_go-slow-earn-autonomy]] rule 5: every relaxation is written down,
never vibes.

## What I will now decide myself, without asking

1. **Ship / merge / deploy rulings on work Kam has already ruled in substance**
   — where the decision is "does this meet the bar we agreed", not "do we want
   this". RD-67+68 is the pattern: he ruled 6a, so the deploy is execution.
2. **Ordering and sequencing** across the fleet — which agent works what, in
   what order, what gets parallelised.
3. **Accepting or refusing an agent's deviation** from a brief on technical
   grounds, and scoring the run.
4. **Rejecting work that does not meet the bar**, and commissioning the refine
   round, without checking first.
5. **Whether a finding is severe enough to interrupt other work.**

## What still stops for Kam, and why I am not treating the grant as covering it

- **Production.** Never-touch-prod is not mine to relax. `datasec-sales-portal-rg`
  in particular is live Vision production inside an otherwise agent-workable
  environment.
- **Money** — spend, credits, licences, anything with a bill attached.
- **External comms to humans** — clients, Peter, Stuart, HP. A wrong word from
  me lands on a real relationship and cannot be recalled.
- **Anything irreversible** where a wrong call cannot be undone in-session:
  deleting credentials or data, destructive registry/tenant operations, force
  pushes, contractual commitments.
- **Scope changes** — new work Kam has not asked for, or work that changes what
  a project IS.
- **Anything where I would be the sole author of the authority.** If I cannot
  point to a Kam ruling or a rule he set, the decision is not execution, it is a
  decision — and it goes to him.

## How to apply

- Decide, act, and **report what I decided** — the grant removes the pause, not
  the receipt. He should always be able to reconstruct why afterwards.
- When genuinely unsure which side of the line something falls, escalate once
  with options and a recommendation. The cost of an unnecessary ask is minutes;
  the cost of an unauthorised irreversible action is trust.
- Revisit at consolidation: if I am escalating almost nothing, the boundary may
  be drifting and needs saying out loud again.
