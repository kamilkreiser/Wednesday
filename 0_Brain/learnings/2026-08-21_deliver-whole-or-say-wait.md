# Deliver whole, or say "wait" — never hand over a deliverable with a hole that closes in minutes

**The lesson:** when a deliverable for Kam depends on something pending (a
ticket number, a link, a result minutes away), do NOT deliver it with a
placeholder and a promise to patch it. Say plainly: "hold — you're waiting on
X; the finished version comes when X lands," then deliver ONCE, complete.

**What triggered it:** the Stuart WhatsApp redraft went out with "KS-###" and
a promise to supply the number — a message he could not send, costing his
attention twice. He was explicit: waiting is fine WHEN TOLD what he is
waiting for.

**How to apply:**
1. Before handing over any artefact, ask: can he USE this as-is? A
   placeholder anywhere in the payload means no → hold + name the wait.
2. The wait notice is one line: what's pending, roughly when, and that the
   complete version arrives then. That line IS the deliverable in the interim.
3. Exception: when he explicitly asks for the partial now, or the gap is
   his to fill by design (options awaiting his choice are not holes).
4. Same family as [[2026-08-06_bluf-write-for-the-reader]] (his attention is
   the scarce resource — a two-touch delivery spends it twice) and
   [[2026-08-07_a-promise-is-not-a-mechanism]] (the "I'll fill it in" tail
   is an intention riding on a deliverable).
