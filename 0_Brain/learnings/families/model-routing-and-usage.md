# Model routing and usage

**The one idea.** Which worker takes a task is a written, measured decision read from one shared table, not a default and not a memory. The weekly usage gauge shapes that choice but never stops the work: Kam's ruling is that nothing happening because of a rule he made is the worst outcome. Every launch says which model, which row and what the gauge read.

## The rules

1. **When I am about to launch a seat, gate or drafter, or route a task: open `2_Project_Files/fleet/specs/model-routing.md`, run its checks, take the row, and write the receipt line** `model: <id from models_latest.json> -- <row> -- <P1-P4> -- <gauge %>`. "Opus for every agent" is lifted. Tier-2 gates (tests, docs, config, tooling, already-gated follow-ups) run on Sonnet; tier-1 gates, security design and data-destroying builds stay on Opus. Model ids are read from `0_Brain/dashboard/data/models_latest.json` at launch, never typed from memory. `2026-10-18_model-routing-v10-approved-route-by-the-table.md`, `2026-10-08_sonnet-trials-and-a-shared-model-routing-definition.md`
2. **The routing definition lives in that one file,** versioned, each line carrying its evidence, shared by all three seats and holding no client content. A model not yet measured on a class of work is a trial there: start where a failure is cheap (drafters, then raising already-gated work, then merges), climb one notch at a time on evidence, and measure claimed savings instead of repeating an estimate. A seat of any model never merges without the QA gate. `2026-10-08_sonnet-trials-and-a-shared-model-routing-definition.md`
3. **Weekly usage is a guide, never a stop (all seats; this is the newest ruling and it wins).** Under 70% route by the table. Past 70% tilt harder to the cheaper tiers (Sonnet, and the local model where a seat has one); Opus stays where the table needs it. No launch is refused for usage alone when a deploy, progress or an outcome needs it: report the gauge in the receipt and do not wait on it. Read the gauge in the same action as the decision. The 90% hard stop of `2026-09-14_at-90pct-weekly-usage-no-new-agents-wednesday-plus-local-model.md` and the 80 to 90% band of `2026-10-14_80-to-90pct-limited-cloud-wednesday-and-spark.md` were both turned into guides by `2026-10-22_usage-is-a-guide-outcomes-over-rules-route-by-learnings.md`: the newer wins.
4. **What still holds from the older usage lessons, as guidance (Wednesday seat).** When the gauge is high, close the cheap obvious items first (gated work merging, record defects, true duplicates, fix rounds on existing PRs); Wednesday and the local model do what they can themselves first; a cloud launch at 80% or more says in its receipt why they cannot (for example a repo write, a gate, box rights). When Kam restates a limit at a given reading ("minimal but used when necessary"), that word holds until its event: the allowance renewal, an account switch, or his word. `2026-09-14_at-90pct-weekly-usage-no-new-agents-wednesday-plus-local-model.md`, `2026-10-14_80-to-90pct-limited-cloud-wednesday-and-spark.md`
5. **When I am unsure, or a rule seems to say stop: do not stop silently.** Post a question card on fleet activity with a default and carry on with the next thing. The coordinator owns the daily task and workload plan and says plainly when she disagrees with Kam. `2026-10-22_usage-is-a-guide-outcomes-over-rules-route-by-learnings.md`, `2026-10-22_tuesday-sonnet-default-usage-is-a-guide.md`
6. **Friday seat:** do not hold back Claude seats at 70%; launch what the work needs, code-partitioned and gated. The lesson names 90% as this seat's hard stop; read it together with rule 3. Signature classes, the QA gate and deploys are untouched. `2026-10-01_friday-cloud-threshold-90.md`
7. **Tuesday seat (Datasec, no local model):** the local tiers collapse into Sonnet. Sonnet first for one-to-three-file spelled-out fixes, test-only pins, raising PRs, merges on a GO, non-decision multi-file builds, drafters and tier-2 gates. Opus first for auth, credential, security or money design, data-destroying paths, deploys with box rights, tier-1 gates and coordination itself. Two measured Sonnet failures on one item send it to Opus. On that machine the binding limit is load, not usage: pace launches. When the ordered local-model machine arrives, re-read the table; assume nothing. `2026-10-22_tuesday-sonnet-default-usage-is-a-guide.md`
8. **Wednesday seat, local model:** the local model is a permanent part of the workflow and the first routing stop for the simplest work (one file, a spelled-out fix, a checker that can fail); Claude seats take the rest. The routing judgement is Wednesday's and each decision names its reason in one clause. A failed local task gets the original brief plus ONE rebrief, then goes to a Claude seat: this is a counter, not a diagnosis, and I do not reason past it. Reallocate "when it makes sense": batched onto a seat already open on that project, never one seat per ticket, and never leaving the local model idle meanwhile. A brief defect is still fixed as a brief defect. Measure the predicate weekly (passed, reallocated, how many reallocations were really brief defects). Where a countable rule and a judgement rule give the same answer, prefer the countable one. `2026-09-16_local-model-is-long-term-and-claude-takes-what-it-cannot-do.md`
9. **Orchestrate and test everything.** Every worker's output is read at source by the coordinator and gated; local workers never merge (a Claude seat raises, the coordinator GOs). The routine always runs, day and night: when a pool thins, write briefs or widen the harness. One client's content per local task and per run directory; Secuura code may go to local models; nothing says Datasec code may reach the Studio's local model. The Spark tier in these lessons is dead (the machine was returned 2026-11-15): route to the next tier. `2026-09-25_three-tier-routing-ornith-spark-cloud-always-on.md`
10. **Switching a model.** For a trial seat or a flagged gate the lessons say: switch that one seat with `/model` in its pane after launch and tell it by mail which model it runs on; a durable per-launch choice is a change to the project's own launcher, made by that project's agent or Kam. The newest lesson says the coordinator seat's own model is set at launch: recommend a relaunch and never run `/model` in a seat pane, because it once wrote Kam's global default. These two are not reconciled in the lessons; until Kam rules, treat `/model` in a pane as unsafe unless the launcher fix that isolates it has been verified. `2026-10-08_sonnet-trials-and-a-shared-model-routing-definition.md`, `2026-11-16_suggest-when-to-switch-to-the-newest-fable.md`
11. **At boot and before commissioning a task: compare the running model with the newest Fable in `models_latest.json` and suggest to Kam** a switch for the seat, or Fable for a task, when the work is design, architecture, security design or review, a hard diagnosis or real ambiguity. Routine build, mechanical and bulk work stays on the table. Suggest switching back when that work is done. It is a suggestion; Kam rules. `2026-11-16_suggest-when-to-switch-to-the-newest-fable.md`
12. **Friday seat, the new-version project only:** architecture, discovery and design-review agents run on the newest Fable, id read from the file at launch (in-session subagents by the Agent tool's model field). Build, test and gate seats still route by the table. Every launch names this ruling as the reason. Ask before widening. `2026-11-16_architecture-agents-run-on-the-newest-fable.md`

## What a tool already enforces

- `2_Project_Files/fleet/usage_gate.sh`, wired into both launch paths, refuses a launch at or over the cut (default 90%) and when the gauge is missing or stale. It does NOT gate in-session drafter subagents (check the gauge by hand before commissioning), and it has NOT been changed to advisory since the usage-is-a-guide ruling: until it is, a launch that the guide allows passes `WED_USAGE_STOP=100` and names the 2026-10-22 lesson in its receipt.
- Nothing enforces the routing receipt line or the Fable comparison at boot.

## Grants and their limits

- **Routing is delegated to the coordinator** (Wednesday for Secuura and general, Tuesday for Datasec's daily plan); misroutes are hers to own and record. Model choice for a seat itself, and any use of a model outside the table, is Kam's: suggest, do not switch.
- **Friday:** the 70% minimise-cloud line is removed for this seat; no expiry stated.
- **Tuesday:** her own time-scoped no-90%-line grant sits in the time-scoped-grants family.
- All of these leave the signature classes, the QA gate before every merge, CodeQL on Datasec and client isolation unchanged.

## The mistakes this family keeps catching

- Holding a launch, a gate or a deploy because the gauge is high, when the ruling is that usage guides and outcomes decide.
- Launching on "Opus by default" (or any default) without opening the table.
- Typing a model id from memory.
- Rebriefing the local model a third time because the diagnosis says it will work this time.
- Letting a dead tier (the Spark) or a retired threshold (70%, 80 to 90%, 90% as a stop) steer a decision because an older lesson still says it.
- Changing a model in a way that silently changes it for seats nobody named.
