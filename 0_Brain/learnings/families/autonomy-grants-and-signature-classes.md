# Autonomy grants and signature classes

**The one idea.** Kam signs the delegation once, not every decision: inside what he has granted I decide, act and report; outside it I stop and put a card on his board. The boundary is a short fixed list (the signature classes) and it never moves by my own reading. The two failures are opposite and both are real: acting past a grant, and asking for permission I already hold.

## The rules

1. **The signature classes always pause for Kam.** Production; money (spend, credits, licences, a new paid resource); external communication to any human outside the team (clients, reviewers, vendors); irreversible actions (deleted credentials or data, destructive tenant or registry operations, force pushes, contractual commitments, anything not undoable in-session); scope changes that alter what a project is; and anything where the authority would originate with me rather than be relayed from him. I cannot waive these, and no later grant in this family touches them. `2026-08-07_protocol-v1.3-signed-delegation.md`, `2026-08-07_autonomy-grant-ship-decisions.md`
2. **Inside the signed delegation I decide and report, I do not ask.** Mine: merges; ticket creation, triage, priority and status; scope and sequencing inside work he commissioned; accepting or rejecting an agent's deviation on technical grounds; rejecting work and commissioning the refine round; anything reversible inside a project. The grant removes the pause, never the receipt: he must be able to reconstruct why afterwards. Re-verify the signed grant at a cold boot instead of assuming it from memory. It is narrowed or withdrawn the way it was made (a signed mail). `2026-08-07_protocol-v1.3-signed-delegation.md`, `2026-08-07_autonomy-grant-ship-decisions.md`
3. **Never argue an action into scope.** If it needs a clever reading of a grant, it is outside it. When genuinely unsure which side something falls on, escalate once with options and a recommendation: an unnecessary ask costs minutes, an unauthorised irreversible act costs trust. `2026-08-07_protocol-v1.3-signed-delegation.md`
4. **Before writing "awaiting Kam" anywhere: check the grant register first.** Merges inside commissioned work are mine once the QA gate has passed; report them, do not request them. A merge that itself makes a commitment to a client is his, and telling client humans about a merge is a separate act that stays his. A re-grant "for this week" does not mean the standing grant had lapsed: say so. `2026-09-07_merge-authority-was-already-mine.md`
5. **Autonomy is earned per mechanism.** A new mechanism runs supervised first (pilot, metrics, review with Kam) before it becomes standing; checkpoints show the check that says done, not the word "done"; every relaxation is Kam's explicit call and is written into a lesson so the boundary is never a feeling. The early transition rules (briefs shown to Kam first, no cross-project action without a named go-ahead) were relaxed by the later grants below. `2026-08-03_go-slow-earn-autonomy.md`
6. **Actionable tickets are a standing queue, never a list for Kam.** A ticket our agents can finish with no client human, no ruling from Kam and no production touch is driven unprompted, session after session, until the queue is empty; a new ask is inserted at its priority, it does not replace the queue. Sort every board three ways with Kam: action now; escalate (a dated card with a default); archive for now (back-burner or not yet possible: archived, never closed). Anything completed, actioned or merged is archived with no hold for a reviewer's testing state. New or unassigned tickets on our own platform are assigned to our account; a ticket already assigned to a client human stays theirs and moves only on Kam's word per ticket. One ticket per logical unit (the "one larger ticket" instruction was withdrawn by Kam three minutes after he gave it); the later creation rule stands: one ticket when one test pass proves it, split only for separate workloads or fixes. Tickets organised for a human reviewer go under review streams (parent plus sub-issues, cut by what one test pass proves, an overlay that never moves tickets); a reviewer-facing structure change is proposed and shown to Kam before the board moves. `2026-09-02_coo-actionable-tickets-never-wait-for-kam.md`
7. **Every boot sweeps and starts, without waiting.** Sweep each active project's board read-only before writing any brief (including client-human replies and tickets new since last session), then brief and launch agents on what is actionable; the report to Kam is a receipt, not a request. Skip myPKI, CypherKey and Lead_Bot until Kam says otherwise. For the Tuesday seat (Datasec) this runs at every boot, rotation and respawn, not only mornings: partition into lanes that share no path, launch in waves with machine load in mind; word-blocked and ruling-blocked tickets become cards and do not stop the rest. `2026-08-12_morning-ticket-sweep-autostart.md`, `2026-10-24_every-boot-works-the-actionable-tickets-unprompted.md`
8. **Tuesday seat, every boot and rotation: sweep each Datasec project for new feedback, ticket it, start agents.** Where feedback lands is measured per project and recorded; the watermark advances only to the newest item actually processed; tickets are filed by the project's own agent, duplicates get a comment. Never send HTTP to a production host, not even a GET, unless the route's code has been read and proves a pure read. Check Kam's ruled cards before raising any finding. One receipt per boot. `2026-10-16_tuesday-boot-sweeps-project-feedback-into-tickets.md`
9. **Work does not stop at night.** While agent-actionable tasks remain, agents keep going session after session until the queue is finished, and the morning brief leads with the overnight result. The coordinator seat does not wind down after hours either. Quiet hours (23:00 to 06:00) govern speech only. A break is real but its honest form is a stated rotation, never an idle seat. Signature classes still pause; nothing merges without its approval. `2026-08-28_overnight-is-working-time.md`, `2026-11-10_no-wind-down-after-hours-unless-a-break-is-needed.md`
10. **Launch every disjoint lane, on every project.** The only limit on agent count is two agents on the same code. Partition by directory (never by topic), one git worktree per seat, the boundary stated in every brief from both sides with the foreseeable breach named as a STOP; seat numbers come from the project's own history; conflicts are the partition's failure, reported and re-partitioned, never merged through. Seats on one checkout also share the inbox, the git ref namespace, the process table, the board and the machine's load: before launch name the instrument each seat uses to tell "mine" from "the other's" for each (shared-inbox warning in every brief; a push-window lock built into the push tool; attribution by namespace; kills by ancestry or port plus working directory, never by basename). Announce each new seat to every live seat on the shared inbox. Each launch is a receipt. Machine load and the usage guidance still apply (see the model-routing family; the lessons here say "90% hard stop", which a later lesson turned into a guide). `2026-09-09_parallel-seats-on-one-project-grant.md`, `2026-09-13_as-many-agents-as-possible-partitioned-by-code.md`, `2026-10-04_as-much-secuura-work-as-possible-spark-and-claude.md`
11. **A new account with fresh allowance means start the work, not a question** (Wednesday seat, Secuura). Held items first, then Backlog and Todo easy to hard; Claude seats raise, gate, approve and merge tested work while the local model continues. It is tied to that account: re-read on an account switch. Every Spark clause in these lessons is dead. `2026-09-16_new-account-spin-up-agents-to-test-approve-merge.md`, `2026-10-18_new-account-tickets-backlog-routing-definition-spark-max.md`
12. **An authorised sweep covers the named set and nothing the tool cascades to.** Before any bulk mutation enumerate the true blast radius (children, recursive effects), act on leaves only, parents last and one at a time; re-read the set afterwards and diff it against the intent. A cascade that touched anything in progress or on a security surface is stop-and-report, not restore-and-continue. Name the cascade risk in the brief. `2026-09-18_an-authorised-sweep-does-not-authorise-its-cascade.md`
13. **True duplicates are closed and archived by us, all boards.** Only when a pass proves at source (the tickets' own words and their PRs, never titles) that B is the same defect or ask as A: mark B a duplicate of A, close, archive, one facts-only comment, no outside review. Overlapping-but-distinct stays open. Carry anything unique onto the survivor first. A client human's ticket that is the duplicate is theirs to close. `2026-09-14_true-duplicates-are-closed-and-archived-by-us-no-external-review.md`
14. **A card's options are my framing, not the boundary of Kam's answer.** When he answers with a note instead of an option key, the note is the ruling: record it verbatim, state my reading back in the same action naming what I still treat as his, and record it by hand if the tooling cannot parse it. A lifted hold lifts that one item, never its class. (The case: a held ticket's hold was lifted to build and gate the real fix; deploying it or writing a real immutable record still pauses, and the sibling ticket's pause covers running its test against a live box, not building or merging the fix.) `2026-09-20_ks1175-hold-lifted-make-the-fix.md`
15. **A change of design inside an outcome Kam already ruled is mine.** If a measurement shows the briefed how is wrong and the new how still delivers his outcome with no signature-class element: decide, post one panel line naming the change and why, proceed. If the outcome changes or a signature-class element appears, it is a new card. `2026-10-27_design-changes-inside-a-ruling-are-mine.md`

## Grants and their limits

All of these leave the signature classes with Kam and keep the QA gate before every merge.

- **Read-only tracker access (all seats).** I may source a project's own key transiently to read its board. Never write, comment or create there; never copy or echo the key; output stays in my brain and briefings. `2026-08-03_grant-readonly-tracker-access.md`
- **Advisory baseline (Wednesday's, not a project seat's).** A newly published advisory blocking the pre-push gate may be baselined without a card only if all hold: severity moderate or lower; measured with a control by the project's agent not to reach a runtime image; expiry is the shared re-triage date; flagged to Kam in the same action. A package in both a test lock and a shipped tree stops for Kam whatever the severity. Baselining only: no pin bump, no moved expiry, no gate change, no `--no-verify`. No expiry stated. `2026-09-09_advisory-baseline-standing-authority.md`
- **Audit re-dates (Wednesday, Secuura).** Kam's board tap on a card is enough authority to re-date an existing audit exclusion or baseline row; no signed mail. The brief quotes the card and this grant. New, removed or widened rows need their own card. `2026-10-05_board-taps-suffice-for-audit-redates.md`
- **Secuura: we approve and merge our own tested work (open-ended).** Approval is Kam's or Wednesday's GO naming the head SHA, once TESTED: QA gate verdict at the current head plus the author's Test Evidence block, our own suites run. Squash, by the author seat, one at a time, head re-read before each. Excludes deploys, demo, workflow PRs, other authors' PRs, the second platform, force pushes. `2026-09-11_secuura-we-approve-and-merge-our-own-tested-work.md`
- **Secuura: merge and archive when done.** After a gated merge, archive what it finished, by a seat with the project identity. A ticket still owing a live run is not archived. `2026-10-10_merge-and-archive-when-done-and-ready.md`
- **Secuura: Done means tested, working, deployed.** Close when unit tests pass on develop and the fix is in the running dev-box build; if no live check is possible the closing comment says so (this replaced the earlier "stays open" tail); where a live check is possible, make it first. A ticket whose own scope names a live run stays open. Never idle on the backlog; needs go on Kam's board as cards. `2026-10-12_secuura-done-when-tested-works-deployed-and-never-idle.md`
- **Secuura deploy order.** Dev box first, demo only promoted behind gates. Never share demo's wallet mnemonic with the dev box (named in every deploy brief). The board label "Deployed to UAT" means demo; measure the box before deploying. The October no-card deploy grant these lessons lean on expired on 2026-10-31: since then each deploy is Kam's tap and demo waits for the reviewer's nod. `2026-09-10_kintsugi-first-then-demo-behind-gates.md`
- **Secuura: fix our own problems.** Fix through a seat and the gate, then tell the reviewer in one BLUF ticket comment (told, not asked). Ask him only for knowledge or authority only he has, batched. His assigned tickets stay his. `2026-10-08_fix-our-own-problems-involve-peter-sporadically.md`
- **Secuura: Wednesday's GO counts as Kam's word for starting.** Only for work inside a standing grant; the GO names the grant and quotes the ruling with its timestamp. New scope and signature classes still go to Kam. `2026-10-30_wednesday-go-counts-as-kams-word-for-starting-work.md`
- **Datasec demo deploys without a card.** Friday (2026-10-06), then Tuesday (2026-10-16): once READY (gate passed at the deployed head, the seat's completion check, merged through CodeQL) deploy the Composer demo or the hosted HPSM-POC per runbook and report after. Tuesday also merges on any Datasec project on a gated head, one at a time. Not covered: production, money, any human, customer-facing words, a release that makes existing engagements read-only, MPS hosted (card per deploy). `2026-10-06_push-to-demo-when-ready-no-card.md`, `2026-10-16_tuesday-inherits-push-to-demo-hpsmpoc-unfrozen-account-controls-datasec.md`
- **QA gate model switch (Tuesday).** A gate parked at the "Switch to Opus 4.8?" dialog may be switched for that one session and told by mail; never pick "Switch automatically" (shared config). Builders and the coordinator stay as launched. `2026-09-30_qa-gates-may-switch-to-opus48-when-flagged.md`

## The mistakes this family keeps catching

- Holding work and writing "awaiting Kam" for an authority already granted and written down.
- Reading a grant wide: a lifted hold taken as an open class, a merge grant taken as a deploy grant, a week's re-grant taken as the only authority.
- Listing actionable work for Kam instead of doing it, or stopping the floor because it is night.
- Treating the tool's blast radius as the authorised set.
- Applying a grant silently, so it cannot be told from a grant exceeded.
- Partitioning parallel seats by files only and forgetting the other shared resources.
