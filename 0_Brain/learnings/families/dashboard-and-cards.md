# The dashboard and decision cards

**The one idea.** The board is the surface Kam reads and rules from, so it must hold exactly what is live: every decision he owes as a card with a default, nothing he has already answered, nothing already done. A card or tile is a representation; what makes it true is the store behind it and the artefact the ruling landed in, and keeping those in step is the coordinator's job in the same action, never a later tidy-up.

## The rules

1. **When a new surface or integration is designed (dashboard, channel, view): it is additive, never substitutive.** It extends the existing system and must not replace or fork it: same underlying truth in files, views generated from them, no state that lives only in one surface, so Kam can switch interaction mode at any moment. The dashboard is an instrument the coordinator plays (curate flags, add sections, drive the day through it), not a report she produces. For access, propose an API or other structured channel first and name the exact one-time action Kam must take; raw credentials are the last resort. Overnight work delivered for the 06:00 briefing is welcome. `2026-08-05_life-os-commission-principles.md`
2. **When I need a decision from Kam: make a card at the moment the decision exists, never only in chat, a terminal reply or a status summary.** Use `2_Project_Files/tools/decision_queue.sh add` (it writes the local queue and posts to the live board's fleet-activity panel). One decision per card: project-prefixed id, client and project, title, a bottom-line-first summary, at least two options, the recommendation, and the default action (what happens if he never answers, and who acts), so nothing blocks on an unanswered item. Chat may point at cards; it is not where the decision lives. He rules in batches on his own schedule. The lesson was given to the Friday seat ("now and going forward"); the same rule for the other seats sits in the Kam-communication family. `2026-09-24_decisions-go-on-the-fleet-activity-panel.md`, `2026-08-21_decision-queue-and-rotation-rhythm.md`
3. **Before filing a card that asks Kam to decide or to commission a measurement: search for the answer as well as the ruling.** Look in his ruled cards on the subject (`decision_queue.sh list ruled`) and in `0_Brain/reference/` for a dated measurement already on disk. A ruled card with no delivery mark is a claim about paperwork, not about whether the work was done. A measurement found undelivered is marked delivered (naming the artefact) and used in the same action; it does not go back to him as a question. If its recommendation is still unbuilt, that is work to claim and carry as owed, not a card. `2026-09-11_before-carding-a-measurement-check-it-has-not-already-run.md`
4. **When the card tool refuses a card for a prior ruling: treat it as a question.** Read each match; if it is a different subject sharing generic words, re-add with the override stating the measured difference. Never name another client's cards in the override text. `2026-09-24_decisions-go-on-the-fleet-activity-panel.md`
5. **When Kam taps or answers a card: rule it in the store in the same step as acting on it.** His taps arrive as chat messages and do not change the store. Run `decision_queue.sh rule <id> <key>`, act, then mark it `--delivered <id> "<artefact>"` once the ruling is in its artefact. Ruled cards come off the board (the rule step hides the tile; if the hide fails it says so, and I re-run it by hand). At every checkpoint compare the open list with what he can see; cards left open after he tapped make him tap again. `2026-10-12_clear-fleet-activity-once-answered-as-a-rule.md`, `2026-09-24_decisions-go-on-the-fleet-activity-panel.md`
6. **When I answer a seat's QUESTION mail: clear its fleet-activity flag in the same step the answer is verified delivered.** Order: send the answer, tap the pointer, then `2_Project_Files/tools/fleet_ack.sh answered "<subject substring>" "<label>"` (a no-match result means re-run after the collector ticks), then the note quoting the real output. At every checkpoint `fleet_ack.sh list` must read zero flagged unless a question is genuinely open. Only the coordinator's session writes "answered", and only after delivery is verified. `2026-10-12_clear-fleet-activity-once-answered-as-a-rule.md`
7. **When a dashboard item (ticket, flag, parking-lot entry) is completed, superseded or expired: move it off in the same session.** Verify completion at the source first (the board, the project history, a receipt), never from my own notes. Move, do not delete: it goes into a dated "Completed (moved off the dashboard)" section of the same card with the verification named, and the card's updated date is bumped. This is part of every morning sweep and every wrap. A tile carrying dead items gets discounted wholesale, and the discount lands on the live ones. `2026-08-20_dashboard-shows-only-live-work.md`
8. **When Kam says he cannot see something on the board: ask for the board's footer line or a screenshot first, and measure the cause in that session.** Zero cards synced means API or auth; cards synced but none needing him means a saved filter, the agent tab or the key on his screen; locked titles mean the key is locked. A post's HTTP 201 proves the server stored the card and nothing more: never use it as evidence that a card "is on the board" in a sentence to him. After any change to how cards are posted, confirm on the page which tab shows them (a Friday card belongs under the Friday tab). `2026-09-24_decisions-go-on-the-fleet-activity-panel.md`
9. **When Kam asks how we work (does this rhythm suit you, what would improve it): answer honestly with evidence and costs, never a courtesy answer,** and raise structural friction at consolidations without waiting to be asked. This is the relationship half of the lesson and it stays a conversation, not a procedure. On rotation the lesson fixes one thing that still holds: 50% context is a checkpoint only (sweep, declare defaults, start nothing heavy), never a rotation trigger. Its rotation point of 70% has since been moved by the context-and-rotation family; take the band from there. `2026-08-21_decision-queue-and-rotation-rhythm.md`

## What a tool already enforces

- `2_Project_Files/tools/decision_queue.sh` refuses to add a card when a prior ruling on the same subject matches, which catches a re-asked question. It does not look in `0_Brain/reference/` for a measurement already on disk, and it cannot tell a ruled card whose work was done from one that was never delivered: both searches in rule 3 are still mine.
- No index row names an enforcing tool for the clearing rules. The lesson records a helper on the Tuesday seat (`2_Project_Files/tuesday/tools/tapped_but_open.sh`, run at the end of the rulings read) that prints tapped-but-open cards with the exact command; when it prints anything, rule those cards before any other step.

## The mistakes this family keeps catching

- A decision left in chat, a handover or a status list, where Kam does not rule from.
- Acting on his tap and leaving the card open, so he taps it again hours later.
- Re-asking a question he already answered, or carding a measurement that already ran.
- Calling a card "on the board" because the post returned success, when his screen shows none.
- A tile still showing finished work, so the live items beside it stop being believed.
- A new surface that quietly holds its own state and forks the truth.
