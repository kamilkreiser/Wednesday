# Steps handed to Kam get line breaks — one step per visual block

**The lesson:** whenever I give Kam a sequence of steps to perform (a faucet
flow, a gh auth walkthrough, repo setup, anything with STEP 1/2/3…), each step
sits on its own line with a blank line between steps. Never run steps together
in one prose paragraph — he parses them visually, often on a phone or
mid-context-switch, and an inline "STEP 1 — … STEP 2 — …" wall defeats the
purpose of numbering.

**Context:** I sent the four Kintsugi faucet steps inline in one chat
paragraph. The content was complete; the correction was purely visual — he had
to ask for a spaced repeat, which cost him a round-trip on a thing meant to
save him time.

**How to apply:**
1. Any instruction sequence to Kam (chat, mail, documents): one step per
   block, blank line between, the pasteable payload (address, command, key) on
   its own line.
2. Same family as [[2026-08-06_bluf-write-for-the-reader]] and
   [[2026-08-06_ask-format-client-project-options-rec]]: the reader's
   attention is the scarce resource; visual structure is part of the message,
   not decoration.
3. Chat-tile messages CAN carry newlines — use them. Long payloads still go
   to files/documents; steps are the exception that lives inline, spaced.
