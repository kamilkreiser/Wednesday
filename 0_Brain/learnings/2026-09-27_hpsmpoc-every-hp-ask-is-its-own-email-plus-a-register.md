# HPSM-POC: every thing Kam needs from HP gets its OWN email to Kam, formatted to copy, and one register document lists them all with what each is for

**His words, verbatim:** *"send me another email with the request. each thing that I need from HP, send me a seperate email with the ask and put together a document with all the requests (with the formatted requests) and what for. Do this as a rule for the project"*

**The operative case, so the headline matches it:** Friday (or a seat's finding relayed by Friday) has something only HP can answer or supply for Datasec/HPSM-POC: corrected content, a permission, a price, approved examples, a confirmation. **Kam sends everything to HP himself** (external communication is his signature class). So Friday's job is to hand him the ask ready to send.

## How to apply
1. **One ask = one email to Kam** (his Datasec address, sent from Friday's own inbox), never bundled with a different ask. The email has a short note for Kam at the top, then a clearly marked COPY FROM HERE / COPY TO HERE block written in Kam's voice for HP, with no internal names, tooling, seats or other clients in it.
2. **The register** `0_Brain/reference/2026-09-27_hpsmpoc-hp-requests/HP_REQUESTS_REGISTER.md` lists every HP ask for this project: id, what we need, what it is for (which ticket, screen or decision it unblocks), the formatted request (the same text as the email), the status (drafted / emailed to Kam / sent to HP / answered), and dates. Update it in the same action as each email.
3. **Asks that already exist count too.** When the register is first built, include asks already made (for example the customer maturity questions Kam requested on 2026-09-25), marked with their true status. An item not yet asked because it still needs Kam's own decision is listed as a candidate, not emailed.
4. **Scope: HPSM-POC** ("as a rule for the project"). Do not generalise it to other projects without his word.
