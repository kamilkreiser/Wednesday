The forwarded-proof test that checks the web front end's value is gone from
the request failed now and then. Its watch filter wrote its reading after the
app's pipeline returned, but TestServer hands the response back once it has
started (the 404 body is written inside the pipeline), so the test read a
field holding the previous request's reading, or none. The gate itself is not
involved: it decides per request from that request's own headers and holds no
state shared between requests.

The filter now hands each reading over through a channel and the test awaits
it; the assertions are unchanged, and a 30 second guard only turns a reading
that never comes into a failure. A new test holds the filter after the
pipeline until the response is back, forcing the late finish on every run.

Runs: target test alone 11 of 20 red before, 20 of 20 green after; with a
250 ms delay planted after the pipeline 10 of 10 red before, 10 of 10 green
after; the new test in its field-reading form 10 of 10 red, 10 of 10 green
now. Test only; no product file changes.
