Each unit box beside a revenue calculator input (reps, customers, devices,
%, USD / hour, hours, USD, USD / device / month) is now one fixed, slim width:
5.375rem (86 px), never shrunk, centred, with 11 px text that may take a second
line (only USD / device / month needs it). The control column keeps its
existing 14rem, so the number box is 138 px wide, 1.6 times its unit box, and
the labels and hints keep the width they had. Before, the box had a maximum
width and no width, so each took its own width, the long ones wrapped to three
lines (one grew the row to 50 px) and the boxes started at different left edges.

Measured in a real browser on the offline mock (steps 1, 3 and 4, at 1280 px):
before, 16 boxes from 28.1 to 85.0 px wide; after, all 16 are 86.0 px wide and
44.0 px tall with one left edge per step, hints 203.2 px wide as before, and no
label or hint wraps differently from before (0 of 96 rows differ across 1280,
820 and 390 px).

No label, hint, unit string, number or default changed. A unit test pins the
width, no-shrink, text size, line height and centring classes, the 14rem column
and the unit text on all 16 boxes; a browser layout test measures widths, lines,
heights, left edges, room round the text, the number box ratio and the hint
width at 1280 and 390 px. Styling only; nothing deployed.
