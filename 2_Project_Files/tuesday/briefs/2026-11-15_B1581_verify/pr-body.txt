## What changes

The policy PDF cover's Date line named the calendar day in UTC, so a release made before 10 am Sydney time showed the previous day. Per the product owner's 2026-10-12 ruling (COMP-15 (a): brand profile, Sydney time, cover only):

- `BrandProfile.document` gains `time_zone` (an IANA zone id); the platform default brand sets `Australia/Sydney`.
- New `dateWordsInZone(iso, timeZone)` in `policy-summary.ts`: the day, month and year numbers come from `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in that zone; the month word still comes from `MONTHS`. A zone the platform refuses throws `RenderRecordError`; it is never read as UTC.
- The cover's two Date values lose ", UTC": `<date> (released)` and `<date> (draft generated)`. No other customer word is added or changed.

## What does not change

- The executive summary's first sentence still reads the UTC day and says `(UTC)`.
- The appendix keeps the ISO instants (`Released at`, `Generated at`).
- The HPSM Preview keeps its UTC clock.
- `dateWords` is byte-for-byte unchanged.
- Output bytes compared at base and head (five inputs, three host time zones): only page 1's Date line of the policy PDF differs; the Preview, DOCX and evidence package bytes are equal. The `brand_profile_hash` of the default brand moves (new field); nothing compares a stored output's hash with a fresh render's, and stored outputs keep their old hash.

## Known and accepted

For a release at 20:00 UTC on 3 October the cover says 4 October while the summary still says "released on 3 October 2026 (UTC)". That is the ruling's own scope (cover only).
