The cover's Date line named the calendar day in UTC, so a release made before
10 am Sydney time showed the previous day. The brand profile now carries a time
zone (Australia/Sydney on the platform default) and the cover reads its date in
that zone. The cover's words lose ", UTC" and keep the rest: "(released)" and
"(draft generated)".

Cover only: the executive summary's sentence still reads the UTC day and says
so, the appendix keeps the ISO instants, and the Preview keeps its UTC clock.
A time zone the platform does not know is refused with a render error, never
read as UTC.
