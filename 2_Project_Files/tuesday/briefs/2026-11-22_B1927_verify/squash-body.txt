DeleteUnreadable now removes a record that does not open through a
private RemoveUnopened: when the record's payload names a key of the
verified ring that is not revoked, a 32-byte random probe must make a
protect and unprotect round trip under the current key first, else
StorageFailed and nothing is removed (cell U22: a good record was
removed when the store's own protection, not the record, failed). A
record under a key the ring does not hold, or holds only as revoked,
is removed as before (U04 and U13 unchanged).

A start over a partition folder that is a symbolic link is refused
with StorageFailed (R18). FileSecretRecordStore checks its folder by
path at the top of TryRead, Write, Delete and ListIds, so a record
folder swapped for a link under a running store makes every record
call StorageFailed (R19). Both checks are by path: a link swapped in
between the look and the file operation is not seen (README limit).
R17 and G03 pin two behaviours that were right but untested: a start
over a revocation alone and no record is refused and makes no key, and
the RevokeOlderKeys guard reads every record.
