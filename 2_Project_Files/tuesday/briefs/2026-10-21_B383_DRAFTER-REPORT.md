## Design notes for Tuesday
- The key trap, written into the brief: an in-one-transaction rebuild runs `DROP TABLE` with foreign keys ON (the PRAGMA is a no-op inside a transaction, B267 measured), so cascades and `RESTRICT` fire. It is atomic and green on every EMPTY test database and can destroy child rows on a POPULATED one. Hence criterion (c): a populated-database proof with child rows and `PRAGMA foreign_key_check`.
- The crash sweep (crash before and after every command, then a recovery `Migrate()`) is one harness that serves both Done-when branches: it MEASURES the windows (branch 1) and is the red-at-base test for the fix (branch 2).
- My one-line view: **not achievable cleanly without a new migration or an out-of-lane run-time change.** The Up bodies hold only EF operations, EF's SQLite provider owns the two-transaction rebuild, and an in-transaction rewrite needs foreign keys on during DROP; I expect Route A to hold for at most the migrations whose rebuilt table has no inbound foreign keys with rows, so the likely end is a STOP with O1 (accept, pin, record) recommended.
