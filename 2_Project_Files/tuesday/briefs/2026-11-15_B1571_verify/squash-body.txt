The journey test in the accessibility spec ran under the 30 s default
test timeout. It now has its own 90 s bound, like the other long journeys
in the suite. Test code only: one comment line and one call in
web/e2e/a11y.spec.ts; no product file, nothing deployed.

Measured alone, the journey test takes 10 to 13 s (about 11 s median),
so it can pass the default on a loaded machine. At a 5000 ms
command-line default the old spec times out on desktop and tablet and
the new one passes; with 1_000 in place of 90_000 it times out again,
so the in-file bound is what governs. The other two tests keep the
default.
