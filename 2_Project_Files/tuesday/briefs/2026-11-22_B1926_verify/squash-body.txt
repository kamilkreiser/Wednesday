new headings under Limits now state the others as the code stands: the
token holder (StoreRefreshValue during a refresh, an access value sent
once after Forget, vault calls under the serial's lock, a grant that
comes after RefreshTimeout, a 401 after a failed refresh, a Forget whose
vault delete throws, a fresh copy from every TryGet), the callback
credentials (a wrong-length hash, unzeroed reads, the Issue race cell
held by two mechanisms) and the generated reading (no declared map in
the inputs, enum wire names, a printer's reaction UNMEASURED).

that device answers are read strictly as a choice for now, to be looked
at again once a printer's traffic is seen. Whether a late grant should
be kept is named as an open question.
