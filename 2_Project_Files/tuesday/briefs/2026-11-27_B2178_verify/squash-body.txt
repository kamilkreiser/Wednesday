HostRig.BoundedAsync hands a host step a token that one clock cancels
at the bound, waits until the step ends or the token is cancelled, then
asks one question: is the token cancelled? Yes is a TimeoutException
naming the step ("the host's start did not end within 60 s"), whatever
order the step's end and the bound came in; no passes the step's own end
on unchanged.

HostRig.StartAsync runs the host's start through it, and DisposeAsync
runs the host's stop and its disposal through it as one step, each at
60 s, so every landed Server cell that starts a host is bounded with no
line of it changed. Two test seams and a settable bound make it
testable. Cell H17 pins the helper in seven shapes and cell H18 pins
its two uses; every wait of both sits under an outer 20 s wait.

The README of the Jobs transport, the OXPd 1.x client and the lab tool
each say that their handler sets no certificate chain policy, so
nothing there turns certificate downloads off, and that a fetch during
the handshake is UNMEASURED on every platform.

Not bounded: a call that blocks its thread (the hang met three times on
macOS arm64 has that shape; its cause is UNMEASURED), the work before
the host's start and the folder delete after its stop. No device has
been reached. Nothing is deployed; nothing was sent.
