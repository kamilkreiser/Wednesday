Three follow-ups to the OXPd 2 contracts, all made in the generator
(contracts/oxpd2/generator). Five files under
src/Datasec.Suite.Adapters.Oxpd2/Generated are regenerated from it and
none is edited by hand. Tests are in tests/Datasec.Suite.Contracts.Tests
(5 new tests; the suite totals 899).

Read-only lists. Oxpd2Schema.Properties, Oxpd2Schema.EnumValues,
Oxpd2InboundOperation.RequestContentTypes and
Oxpd2InboundCatalogue.Operations now each return one ReadOnlyCollection
made once, and the parser reads the private arrays. Before this, a
cast-and-write in the process could change the result of every later
parse. A reflection walk over the runtime, the catalogue and every
reachable schema fails if any public list-shaped member can be written
to; a second cell shows that a cast-and-write is refused and that a
later parse gives the same result.

A bounded body limit. Oxpd2ParseLimits refuses a maxBodyBytes above
Oxpd2ParseLimits.HighestMaxBodyBytes (System.Array.MaxLength - 1) with
ArgumentOutOfRangeException. This is a behaviour change: a limit such
as int.MaxValue - 1, accepted before, is now refused when the limits
are made. A client at the highest accepted limit reads a small answer
without an exception; such a limit used to overflow.

The Unicode length rule. Row N9 gains astral-character strings at and
past maxLength and minLength. String lengths are counted in Unicode
scalar values, and N9 would now fail if they were counted in UTF-16
units.

Parse results are otherwise unchanged. No HP input, project file, lock
file, package, workflow or solution-file line changes. Nothing is
deployed.
