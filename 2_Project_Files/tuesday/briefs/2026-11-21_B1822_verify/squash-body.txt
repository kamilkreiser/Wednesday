A library (src/Datasec.Suite.Cards, 17 files, no package) and its tests
(tests/Datasec.Suite.Cards.Tests, 108 methods, 218 cells).

Nothing reads a real card reader yet. The library opens nothing, logs
nothing, reads no clock and holds no key. The server does not reference
it. Every golden value is PROVISIONAL: made by reading the source of
the Workpath sign-in app, never generated by it and never measured on a
reader. The golden file says so and a test holds it to that.

Five decoding routines in two families, re-expressed from the app's
logic. The older family: one routine each for readers 3111:15354,
1008:69, and 2520:1040 with 2520:784. The newer family: whole-buffer
text for vendor 1008 and a generic keyboard routine. The two families
do not give the same string for every input (7 of 13 comparison rows
differ). Which family a customer's stored cards came from is not known:
both are built, with three selections and a default that follows the
design sentence. A table of the six reader ids; the two conditional
readers decode nothing unless switched on.

An assembler puts the events of one reader in sequence order, holds an
event ahead of a missing one, and refuses a read with a missing event,
a read that does not end within one window of its earliest event, more
than 256 gathered characters, or an event over 4096 bytes or reports.
The caller passes a time that must never go back; an earlier time is a
step that refuses the read in flight. After a refusal the rest of that
read is discarded until its end or one quiet window, so no part of a
refused read is given out while its events keep arriving less than one
window apart. The cost: a slow or interrupted tap is refused and must
be repeated, and the reader is deaf for up to one window after it. The
named limits are in the code's comments. A result never prints its
card string, and the framework's JSON serialiser leaves it out.

Differs from the app, each a default chosen by the build's coordinator
or by the builder: a UUID loses its dashes in the canonical form only;
a byte above 127 is refused; a two-byte report through the older
RF IDeas routine is refused; a first event numbered 0 is taken; the
assembler's rules are new.

Two lines added to Datasec.Suite.slnx; no package, no workflow line.
The root README lists are updated later. Not tested: anything on a
printer or a card reader. Nothing is deployed; nothing was sent.
