Adds the job service of the OXPd 2 server without the converter (work
package WP1.7, in part). The host does not use it yet: nothing is wired
to an endpoint, and nothing calls Sweep or WipeSession.

JobSpool: an encrypted spool of job files. Each job has its own 32-byte
key, held in memory only and cleared when the job is wiped. Files are
AES-256-GCM chunks whose tag is checked before a byte is handed out; a
changed, cut, extended, reordered or moved file is SpoolCorrupt, and a
reader that refused once refuses every later read. A job is wiped by
WipeJob, WipeSession, a touch after its lifetime, Sweep, and at the
start of a new spool. Caps count the plaintext written and are reserved
across all jobs before a chunk reaches the disk.

ScanReceiver: OpenScan makes a job and a 32-byte scan secret returned
once; only its SHA-256 is kept, compared in fixed time, and every
refusal of the secret or the job is the same JobNotFound. ReceiveAsync
streams one multipart upload into the job within the caps and a time
bound; a failed upload wipes the job. One upload per job at a time.

PrintJobSender: SendAsync builds a Print-Job request from the IPP
library's public types, writes it and the document through an
IIppTransport and accepts only a valid answer to that request;
TrackAsync polls Get-Job-Attributes until the state is final. The send
and the answer's read are bounded.

Every refusal is a JobServiceException with one of 18 reasons and a
fixed message, never carrying a document byte, a secret, a file name or
an id. A transport or answer stream that throws its own cancellation is
TransportFailed. Every bound is armed before a caller's code runs, so a
body, transport or answer stream that blocks its thread ends at its
bound.

Limits: deleting a file does not overwrite its blocks; the key lives in
process memory; no timer (expired files stay until the next touch or
Sweep); a bound cannot free a blocked thread; an answer whose close
never completes ends ResponseTimedOut at the send bound; a caller's body
that throws ObjectDisposedException ends JobNotFound; the test that
looks for the secret's text does not see text kept as the state of a
clock, a task, a cancellation token, source or registration, or an
execution context, and the library keeps none there; one process per
root; the real transport, what devices send and what printers accept
are not here or not measured.

The test that walks the receiver for the secret's text no longer enters
a clock, a task, a cancellation token, source or registration, or an
execution context, so other running tests cannot change a collection
under it.

120 new tests. The solution file gains the two job-service lines; no
package, lock file of another project, workflow or build file changes.
Nothing is deployed; nothing was sent.
