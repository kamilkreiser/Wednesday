## The hand-off row (round 3)

- It does not pin the thread pool. A pin made it fail on unchanged code under machine load, so it asserts order, not starvation.
- It prints `slack2` and `slack3` (the margins to the 2 s bound), the CPU count, the pool minimum and the thread counts on one clock. The order assertion, the Busy words and the 2 s bound are unchanged.
- The held stores are released at 1350 ms, not 1500 ms. At 1500 ms the third steps' margin fell under 250 ms in 13 of 20 unpinned runs (worst 172 ms). At 1350 ms the worst margin over 100 runs was 278 ms, at machine load up to about 62.
