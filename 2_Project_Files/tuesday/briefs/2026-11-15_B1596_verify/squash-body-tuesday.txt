Tests only, no product change. Adds three test files under
tests/MpsCalc.Api.Tests/ for the source lock waits of MPSCALC-99 (its
clause 5 and the held review findings):

- LaneMAParkedPool.cs: a helper that parks pool threads and reads back
  how many are free.
- LaneMAClause5Tests.cs: overlay writers hold while source steps wait on
  the store lock, at 16 and 64, each answering within the bound plus
  500 ms.
- LaneMAPollAndHandOffTests.cs: the async lock-file poll row and the
  hand-off (turn order) row.

The hand-off row does not pin the pool (a pin made it fail on unchanged
code under load). It asserts order, prints slack2 and slack3 (the margins
to the 2 s bound) with the CPU and pool counts, and releases the held
stores at 1350 ms so that both margins stay above 250 ms.
