The hand-off row of the lane M-A timing tests ran with a pinned pool that
left it too few threads on a loaded machine, so it failed on unchanged
code. The pin is removed: the row asserts order, not starvation.

The row now prints slack2 and slack3 (margins to the 2 s bound), the CPU
count, the pool minimum and the thread counts on one clock. No assertion,
word or threshold changed. The release of the held stores moves from
1500 ms to 1350 ms to keep both margins above 250 ms.

Tests only, one file, no product change.
