BLUF: Reading the risk-conditions list (GET /api/risk-conditions) no longer rewrites the stored risk conditions for a reader who is not an administrator. The read now runs the sync only when the same administrator check that guards the write routes passes for the request. Everyone else is answered from the stored conditions, silently. Where the check passes for everyone (the open first-run window, sign-in not configured) the read still syncs, as the write routes do. The save route's own sync and the manual sync route are unchanged.
