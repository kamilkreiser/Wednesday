Two rows appended to the 390 px dashboard spec. The first opens the New quote
dialog as a Seller at 390 px and reads the dialog element's own box: its left
edge is at least 0 and its right edge no further than the window's client
width. Both edges are checked because the dialog ends its flex line, so a
widening runs off the left edge while the right edge still equals the client
width. The control sets the dialog's inline min-width to 600 px and expects
the same check to see it. Stub step only; no product file changes.
