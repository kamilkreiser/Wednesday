The dark-mode contrast suite (tests/e2e/dark-mode-contrast.spec.js) carried two allow-list entries that no longer excuse anything, and a light baseline file last measured in early September. This change removes the two entries and regenerates the baseline with the suite's own writer.

## What changes (two files, one commit)
- tests/e2e/dark-mode-contrast.spec.js: the `.consumable-text` entry (RD-175) and the `.badge.destination-badge` entry (RD-180) are replaced by a comment each. Nothing else in the file changes (8 lines added, 6 removed). The `.loading-overlay` entry stays.
- tests/e2e/light-baselines.json: regenerated by the suite under `UPDATE_LIGHT_BASELINES=1`, not edited by hand. 345 -> 218 named failures in the same 23 states; 138 lines left because they now pass (72 tab-button, 36 metric-label, 19 nav-link, 11 others); 11 arrived (the High Risk label of the anomalies tab, a provider link on the agent-roles settings panel, two rate-limit descriptions and five settings hints in the wizard, two example host names on its SSL step). A second regeneration wrote the same bytes.

## Departure from the ticket
The ticket asked to remove the `.consumable-text` entry and "keep the other entries as they are". The dead-excusal guard named `.badge.destination-badge` as well (suppressed nothing in any state of any page), so keeping it would leave the guard red. Removing both was the coordinator's call, under the standing backlog order; it is not a product decision.

## Result (local surface at the base commit, second boot, Chromium via Playwright 1.62.1, both modes)
Plain run: 3 failed, 20 passed. The dead-excusal guard names no entry. Before the change the same surface read 5 failed, 18 passed, with the guard naming both entries.
The three reds are not touched here and stay:
- `dashboard - all 9 states have no unexcused text below 4.5:1` (two dark "High Risk" labels on the anomalies tab, 3.96 and 4.46)
- the dark and the light per-state count guards (`dashboard/anomalies` measured 982, baseline 80 in tests/e2e/state-baselines.json)

The first plain run also had one intermittent failure of `reproduces itself - five runs, byte-identical failure LIST` in the wizard SSL step (the SSL status panel rows were absent in one of five sweeps); the next plain run on a fresh boot passed. It is filed separately.
