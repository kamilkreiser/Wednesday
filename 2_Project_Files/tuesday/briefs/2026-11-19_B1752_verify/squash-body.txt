named Projection under the usage and deal usage tables: six measures down, monthly, annual and contract across, every figure the
server's decimal shown through the existing formatters, with no browser arithmetic. A calculation without a projection shows
nothing of it, and a rental quote is unchanged. The ten words of the table are new and wait for the product owner's words gate
before any deployment.

The dev stub now gives a caller with internal-metrics a zero projection so the leak cell has a payload to test; a caller without
internal-metrics gets no projection key. New vitest rows (nine) and four stub-off browser rows pin the table, its absence and the
placement; the synthetic fixture is deliberately not self-consistent so a browser sum or product would print a different figure.
