A library (src/Datasec.Suite.UniversalPrint, folder Ipp) and its tests
(tests/Datasec.Suite.UniversalPrint.Tests, 137 methods, 284 cells).

Nothing in it talks to anything: no connection, no address, no token,
no key. The server does not reference it yet. Every byte fixture is
PROVISIONAL: built by reading the public encoding standard and the
Workpath app's source, compared with no captured bytes.

The reader takes one IPP packet from a stream and stops at the end
tag. What is not a well-formed packet ends in one exception type. A
packet over 1 MiB is refused; collections nest at most 32 deep; a
value tag it does not know is kept. The delimiters 0x01, 0x02, 0x04,
0x05 and 0x06 to 0x0f start a group that keeps its tag; 0x00 is
refused. Within its bound it may allocate many times the bytes it
reads. The writer refuses a name or a value over 32767 bytes.

Two request builders, Get-Jobs and Acknowledge-Job, with the address
of the service, the device host and the port as parameters and no
default. As in the Workpath app, Acknowledge-Job carries no
printer-uri. Not measured by anyone: the version (2.0), the request
id (1) and the two leading attributes of Get-Jobs. Three name lists
(14 creatable, 30 blocked, 48 watched in five sets), each compared
whole by a test, six success codes, and the difference of two
snapshots.

The document stream asks for a range, requires status 206 and a
Content-Range that starts at the offset asked, drops the IPP packet of
every answer and yields document bytes only, never more than the
total. A failed read is followed by a wait and a new fetch at the
offset reached, three retries at one offset; the waits are functions
the caller hands in and nothing sleeps. Once closed, from any thread,
it fetches nothing, waits for nothing, returns no count and disposes
every answer; a read in flight ends in ObjectDisposedException. One
read at a time: a second read is refused. A body that reports more
than it was asked for is a fetch error. The limits are named in the
stream's comment: a fetch or a wait in flight at a close runs to its
end, a body read that never ends stays pending, and the start of each
caller function is under a short lock that a close also takes. As in
the Workpath app, a failed fetch or a bad answer is not retried and
the IPP status of an answer is not checked. Differs from the app: the
unit word in any letter case, digits only, status 416 as a typed
error, a look at the close before every wait.

Two lines added to Datasec.Suite.slnx; no package, no workflow line.
The root README lists are updated later.

Every choice inside this change was made by the build's coordinator or
by the builder; none is a product decision. Not tested: anything
against a printer, a tenant, the print service or a real HTTP client.
Nothing is deployed; nothing was sent.
