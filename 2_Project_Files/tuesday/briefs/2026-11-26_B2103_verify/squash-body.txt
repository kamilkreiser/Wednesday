body. A register's undo is the unregister of the same id. The writer

unchanged.

UnregisterTopLevelButton with --button-id (and, for a register,
--button-title, --button-target and --button-position); a usage error
before any folder or connection otherwise. An executed register reads
the records first and writes nothing, with no undo, when a record holds
the id in any letter case (exit 10); an answered register or unregister
