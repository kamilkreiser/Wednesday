On the live snapshot card, inside the note's disclosure, the assessor now
chooses how the answer is known from the five existing words (verified,
observed, stated by the customer, assumed, not assessed), under the label
"How do we know?", pre-filled with today's default (stated by the customer).
The choice saves at once with the answer and is read back on return, so the
next save keeps it. The exported report prints the stored basis as it
already does. An answer drawn from an import keeps its own rule (observed
until confirmed, then verified) and shows no choice. The client follow-up
and the partner's review are unchanged. No API, report or wording change.

Web only: the draft carries the basis, toWrite sends it and fromRecords
reads it back; the question card takes an opt-in prop that only the
snapshot runner passes. An uncited answer already stored with a basis other
than "stated by the customer" now keeps that basis on its next save from the
live card.
