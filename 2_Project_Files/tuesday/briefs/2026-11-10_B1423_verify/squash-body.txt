The Expert Discovery screen's typed editor keyed every declared sub-answer
at the engagement level, so a sub-answer whose scope is device_group could
not be answered per group: its one box wrote a draft with no device group,
which the API refuses. Such a sub-answer now has one box per in-scope device
group, named by the group, and its draft and saved answer carry the group's
id (device_group_id). Engagement-level sub-answers, the only kind either
content release declares today, render and save exactly as before.
