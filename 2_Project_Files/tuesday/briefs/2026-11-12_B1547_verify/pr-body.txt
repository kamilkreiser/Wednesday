Why an override, and why those two parents
- The vulnerable copy is a single hoisted minimatch 10.2.6 that eslint, @eslint/config-array (eslint's own dependency) and @typescript-eslint/typescript-estree share. This is not Dependabot lag.
- No stable release of any of the three admits minimatch 11. Registry read on 2026-11-12: eslint has 381 stable versions, @eslint/config-array 20 and @typescript-eslint/typescript-estree 415; 0 of them declare an 11.x minimatch (latest: eslint 10.14.0 `^10.2.6`, config-array 0.23.5 `^10.2.4`, typescript-estree 8.72.2 `^10.2.2`). Only prereleases of typescript-estree (8.72.3-alpha.1 to -alpha.6) declare `^11.0.1`, and a prerelease is not a release. So bumping a direct dependency cannot reach the patched version, and an override is the only mechanism.
- Scoped to eslint and typescript-estree because the other minimatch, 5.1.9 under @redocly/openapi-core, is the OpenAPI generator's exact pin and is outside the vulnerable range. An unscoped override was ruled out beforehand: a trial on a copy (not run again here) showed it also rewrites that exact pin to a different major.
