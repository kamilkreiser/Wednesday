short code and once with a 60-character code that has no break, compares
the page count, the layout overflow and the widths of the two card columns
of the first row (12 pages and 297 px each for both; without the rule the
long code gave 13 pages and columns of 469 px and 125 px). The spec runs in
the stub-off end-to-end step only.

between two lines), removes U+0001 from text nodes and attribute values

in a description, a narrative field and an attribute value, and for a
figure split across two text nodes (88 cells before, 94 after).
