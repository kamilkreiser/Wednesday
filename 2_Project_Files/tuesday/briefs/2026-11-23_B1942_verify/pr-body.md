## What this changes

Four pieces of dashboard text failed the readable-contrast check (WCAG AA, 4.5:1) against the ground they actually sit
on. Each is now measured against that ground, in light and dark mode. Every colour written is a brand token.

| text | mode | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| KPI labels (`.metric-card .metric-label`, written by `static/js/index.js`) | light | `#7f8c8d` on white, 3.48 | `#00719f` on white, 5.44 |
| | dark | `#8c8c8c` on the dark tile gradient, 5.07 to 4.50 | unchanged |
| Import description (`.file-description`) | light | `#7f8c8d` on `#f8f9fa`, 3.30 | `#00719f` on `#f8f9fa`, 5.16 |
| | dark | `#8c8c8c` on `#262626`, 4.50 | unchanged |
| Import drop text (`.drop-text`) | light | `#7f8c8d` on white, 3.48 (3.14 and 3.19 in the drag-over and file-chosen states) | `#00719f` on white, 5.44 (4.90 and 4.98 in those states) |
| | dark | `#7f8c8d` on `#1c1c1c`, 4.90 (it had no dark rule) | `#8c8c8c` on `#1c1c1c`, 5.07, in all three states |
| Toner percentage (`.consumable-text`) | light | white on the `#e9ecef` track, 1.19, with a text-shadow | `#333333` (`--nx-text-strong`) on its own white chip, 12.64, no text-shadow |
| | dark | white on the same light track, 1.19 | `#dedede` on its own `#1c1c1c` chip, 12.67 |

The toner bars are graphics, held to 3:1 against their track (the repository's rule for a bar). In dark mode the track
is now `#262626` (`--nx-surface-raised`, the dark value of the light track's own token) and the black fill is
`#8c8c8c` (`--nx-text-muted`, dark). Lowest stop of each fill on the dark track:

| fill | before (light track `#e9ecef` in dark) | after (dark track `#262626`) |
|---|---|---|
| black | 7.83 | 4.50 |
| cyan | 1.74 | 6.59 |
| magenta | 2.58 | 3.48 |
| yellow | 1.29 | 9.28 |

- `static/css/index-styles.css`: three text colour lines, and the toner label becomes a small opaque chip (16px tall
  inside the 20px bar, centred, never clipped). The text-shadow is removed: no single text colour clears the track and
  all four toner fills, and a shadow is not credited as contrast.
- `static/css/dark-mode.css`: the drop text joins the existing dark muted-text rule; the toner track, the black fill
  and the chip get dark rules. Without the first, the new light colour would have reached dark mode at 3.13.
- `scripts/derive-brand-tokens.js` and `static/css/tokens.css`: the pinned light count of `--nx-text-strong` moves 22
  to 23 for the chip's one new use, with a comment at the pin; `tokens.css` is regenerated by the generator and differs
  in that one count. The palette does not change.
- `docs/BRAND.md`: R5 now says `#00719f` is never text on a DARK ground, and one paragraph records its light text uses
  (5.44 on white, 5.16 on `#f8f9fa`, 4.58 on `#e9ecef`) with the two records it rests on quoted. The count table row for
  `--nx-text-strong` moves 15 to 16 with a note.
- `__tests__/fixtures/index-light-failure-baseline.json`: two signatures leave the recorded light failure set
  (`p.file-description x3` and `span x3`, the drop-text spans); none appears.
- `__tests__/rd349-rd175-readable-text-on-its-ground.test.js`: 19 cells that read the stylesheets and `tokens.css` and
  measure each colour with the repository's own contrast helper against the ground its own rule paints, including the
  drag-over and file-chosen grounds and every toner fill on the dark track. The chip colour must be the
  `--nx-text-strong` token.
- `scripts/verify-expected-counts.json`: 6235 tests in 394 suites.

No script logic changes and no text a user reads changes.

## Who decided

The product owner's recorded outcome: the earlier grey-text decision applies to these three places, and the white
toner text is fixed. It names the result, not the colours. The colours are the coordinator's defaults: `#00719f` for
the three texts (the colour the tab strip already uses), the opaque chip in `--nx-text-strong` with the text-shadow
removed, the dark track `#262626` with the dark black fill `#8c8c8c`, and the drop text in the dark muted-text rule.
