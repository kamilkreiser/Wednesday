PurchaseBomEditor.test.tsx and PriceReason.test.tsx waited on the testing
library's own default of 1000 ms for every findBy*, findAllBy* and waitFor, so
a loaded machine could fail a row on a wait that the row budget never reached.
Every awaited query and wait in the helper and in the rows now carries one
shared WAIT_MS of 10_000 ms, which ends inside the 15_000 ms row budget, and
every row that holds such a wait or calls the helper has that second layer.
