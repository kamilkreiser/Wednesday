Feedback hourly limit (product): a submit stored its item under the store's lock but released its
reservation in a separate locked step, so a concurrent post of the same caller could see that item
counted twice and be refused 429 while under the limit. The reservation is now released in the same
locked step that stores the item; an internal test seam and a new row (red without the change) pin it.
The limit, the counting rule, the 429/503 answers and the 60-minute window are unchanged.

tasks at once could leave a null that the host's disposal dereferenced. Callers of the shared test
host are now made through one locked method. No assertion was removed or weakened.
