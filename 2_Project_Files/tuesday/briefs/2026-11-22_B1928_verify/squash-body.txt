A new library, Datasec.Suite.SignIn, and its test project. SignInActor
is the sign-in state machine of one printer: one channel read by one
loop, so the printer's events are taken one at a time in arrival order.
A card tap follows the card paths: the same card signs its user out with
no lookup, a card of the signed-in user signs out, another user's card
signs the first out and the second in, and an unknown card signs the
signed-in user out and is reported.

A user is signed in on the printer and in the token broker only when the
object id the identity service read from the token equals the card
user's (both parsed as identifiers) and the grant's subject equals the
card user's object id, checked before the printer is told anything.
Otherwise the tap is refused, the Audit event is raised once with no
identity in it, the printer is told and the grant is disposed. A tap
whose age from its arrival to its turn is at least MaxTapAge, or whose
stamp is from the future, is StaleTap and calls nothing; a tap the
printer re-delivers after an outage is a named limit. Every identity and
printer call is bounded by StepTimeout on the actor's TimeProvider, a
newer tap drops an attempt not yet at the printer, and the display name
comes in the four forms with their fallbacks.
