## Summary
Documentation only: two new rows in the calculation register, docs/ENGINE.md. No code, no test and no behaviour changes. Nothing is deployed by this merge.

- MPSCALC-196: row X-33 records the purchase refusals the code already makes. Page estimates are checked on every purchase quote, with or without deal usage. A page estimate on a line that is not a device raises PAGES ON NON-DEVICE LINE. Colour pages on a mono book device raise COLOUR VOLUME ON MONO DEVICE. A line on margin on price at 100% or more raises INVALID MARGIN, naming the line or the section, whichever chose margin. Any of the three blocks the calculation before any figure is formed.
- MPSCALC-147: row X-46 records that colour-panel kits are options. The importer reads a model sheet's colour-panel section as options of that sheet's devices, so a panel picked from the catalogue is class Option, counts in the hardware subtotal by X-23 and X-24, and is costed at its device's pool tier. A manual line keeps the class it is given.
