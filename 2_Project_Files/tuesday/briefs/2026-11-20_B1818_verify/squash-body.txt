Adds the SM 3.16 catalogue as two derived, sha-locked sources under
content/sources/, with their provenance in SOURCES.json, and builds both
releases from them. release-draft goes from 55 to 137 policy items, 55 to 351
fields and 123 to 132 controls (content hash 5679855b...04f104).
release-demo keeps its 55 items and 123 controls; each of them gains its SM
ids and two sources are added, so its hash moves from 05070f1d... to
5db37614...0d2c1d.

The importer refuses an SM id that is not in the catalogue source. The policy
PDF's exception register gets its own text size, and a long cell word may break
at a camel-case boundary. Rendered document text has its own secret-scan
function, renderedTextCredentialShapes, which skips a run of title-cased field
labels. The strict credentialShapes that every input path uses is built from
the same rule expressions as before.

The release is built and hashed but not cut. A deploy of any Composer host from
this commit ships a changed content release and needs the product owner's own
decision first.
