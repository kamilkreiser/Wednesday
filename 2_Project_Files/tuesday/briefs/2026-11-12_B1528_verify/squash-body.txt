The rental short proposal (ProposalView) printed a second page from 9 device lines once the
monthly charge breakdown was on it (12 lines without it), and a third page at 40 lines. It now
shows the first five device lines and, above five, one row that points to the full proposal
("and N more lines (see full proposal)"; "and 1 more line ..." for one). The totals, the
monthly charge breakdown, the options and the software still cover the whole quote. The
purchase short form and all CSS are unchanged.

Limit: only the Devices table is capped. A quote with 4 or more hardware rows and 4 or more
software rows (or very long device names) can still print two pages.

The wording of the pointer row is a slot until the product owner approves it. Nothing is
deployed. Tests: unit rows for the cap, the literal wording and the uncapped totals; print.spec
rows that print 3, 5, 6, 10 and 40 lines to exactly one page (they run in the stub-off CI step
and skip with the stub on); proposal-buttons (local, needs pdftotext) now expects exactly one
page.
