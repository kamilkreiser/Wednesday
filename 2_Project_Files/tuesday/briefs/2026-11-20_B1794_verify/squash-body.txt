Autosave sent one save per typing pause longer than the quiet interval,
and every save writes a history event and a revision (22 in a minute of
paused typing). After a save request, the next autosave now waits until a
minimum gap has passed (a named constant, 10 seconds, beside the quiet
interval); a change made inside the gap is sent by one trailing save when
the gap ends and the quiet interval after the last edit has passed. The
first save of a visit, leaving the screen and every explicit save still
send at once. A change pending inside the gap is sent when the gap ends;
closing the tab inside the gap is covered by the existing unsaved-changes
prompt, as before. The quiet interval, the server and the screen's words
are unchanged. This is the coordinator's default under the ticket's own
text, reversible in one constant.
