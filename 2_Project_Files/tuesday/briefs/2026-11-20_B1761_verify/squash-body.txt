The proposal page of a purchase quote gets a two-choice group beside the print buttons. The priced choice, the default each time the page opens, renders the full proposal and the short form exactly as before. The unpriced choice leaves out every figure the server sent and every sentence, table column, row, tile and callout built only from one; nothing is computed and no word is added to either document. The unpriced document root carries data-price-option="unpriced". The group is not printed. The rental documents are unchanged and the group is not shown on a rental quote.

The three words of the control are pending the product owner's word pack and are not shown on any hosted site until it is ruled. The second choice says SKUs, but the unpriced documents show descriptions and quantities, not part codes. The choice is held by the page only; it is not stored with the quote, so MPSCALC-278 stays open on "the choice is recorded".

A new test file checks the rendered text of both documents over four synthetic fixtures for the figures the server sent in their full display forms, the closed list of omissions and table shapes, and that no unknown text appears. It reads text only: an amount in an attribute or a figure in another format would not be caught by it. A figure staff type into a narrative field is not removed.
