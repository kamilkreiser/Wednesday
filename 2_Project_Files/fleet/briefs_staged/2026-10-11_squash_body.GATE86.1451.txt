## What changes

Six POST operations in three services published a request body that was not marked required, so a spec-driven caller such as Schemathesis may send no body at all: anchoring `POST /api/anchors` and `POST /api/anchors/thread-mint`, staking `POST /api/stake/complete-unstake`, and transfer `POST /api/transfers`, `POST /api/delegations` and `POST /api/delegations/validate`. Each request body now sets `required: true` in its `*.openapi.ts` file. `docs/openapi/secuura-api.yaml` is regenerated by `npm run generate-openapi`, never hand-edited: 6 insertions and 0 removals by `git diff --numstat`, and the same by a hunk read, each a `required: true` under one of the six operations.

**It declares the body required in the published contract; it does not make any handler refuse anything.** No handler or route file is in the diff: `git diff --name-only` lists 9 paths (the three `*.openapi.ts`, three new tests, the yaml, two docs). What each handler does with an absent body today was not measured here. UNMEASURED.

**UNVERIFIED text in the diff.** The header comments of the three new test files state what the handlers do with an absent body (for example, that a handler answers 400). That is the payload author's reading of the handlers. I did not check it, and I committed those comments as received. The test cells themselves assert only the generated document, plus (anchoring) that two request validators refuse `{}`.
