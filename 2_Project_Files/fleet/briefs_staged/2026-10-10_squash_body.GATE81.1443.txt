Three api-gateway routers share a fail500() helper whose log expression was String(err).
A thrown non-Error object therefore rendered as "[object Object]" and its content was lost
from the log. The expression now logs a thrown object's TYPE and FIELD NAMES only, per Kam's
ruling of 2026-09-27, and is byte-for-byte the expression already live in
services/originate/src/routes/adminConfig.ts.

Deliberately unchanged, and not claimed as fixed: a thrown Error still logs its .message, and
thrown TEXT is still logged verbatim. QA gate77 recorded a credential-shaped URL reaching the
log intact; that case stays open on the ticket. The 500 response body is untouched - it was
already the constant INTERNAL_ERROR body before this change.
