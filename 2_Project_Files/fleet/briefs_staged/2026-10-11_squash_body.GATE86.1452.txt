## What changes

Five POST operations in three services published a request body that was not marked required, so a spec-driven caller such as Schemathesis may send no body at all: analytics `POST /api/analytics/exports`; m365-integration `POST /api/teams/webhook-config` and `POST /api/teams/notify`; api-gateway `POST /api/batch/certifications` and `POST /api/batch/delegations`. Each request body now sets `required: true` in its `*.openapi.ts` file. `docs/openapi/secuura-api.yaml` is regenerated by `npm run generate-openapi`, never hand-edited: 5 insertions and 0 removals by `git diff --numstat`, and a structural diff of the base yaml against the new one (js-yaml, 344 operations compared) shows exactly 5 operations changed, each only `requestBody.required` going from undefined to `true`, with `info.version` unchanged.

**It declares the body required in the published contract; it does not make any handler refuse anything.** No handler or route file is in the diff: `git diff --name-status` lists 9 paths (the three `*.openapi.ts`, three new tests, the yaml, two docs). What each handler does with an absent body today was not measured here. UNMEASURED.

**UNVERIFIED text in the diff.** The header comments of the three new test files state what the handlers do with an absent body (for example, that a handler answers 400, or that an absent body "is always refused"). That is the payload author's reading of the handlers. I did not check it, and I committed those comments as received. The test cells themselves assert the generated document, plus that two request validators (the analytics export schema and the m365 notify schema) refuse `{}`.
