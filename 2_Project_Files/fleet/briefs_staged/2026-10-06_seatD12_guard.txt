THE THING THAT MATTERS MORE, AND I GOT IT WRONG IN THE PROJECTION MAIL
I wrote that "a guard trip is the designed STOP" as though the 4,000 MB floor bounds the risk. It does
not, and I should have read the guard before saying so. Read from the box's own copy of the running
script, `.build_d12.sh:25`: the check is `f=$(free_mb); if [ "$f" -lt "$MIN_FREE_MB" ]` — it runs ONCE
BEFORE each build. The free-space sampler at :32 only RECORDS the minimum for the report; it never
stops anything. So the floor is a pre-build admission test, and it cannot protect against a SINGLE image
whose transient dip exceeds the headroom available when that image starts.
