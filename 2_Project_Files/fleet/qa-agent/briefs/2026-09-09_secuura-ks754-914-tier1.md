## 3. WHAT THIS PASS MUST ESTABLISH

Ordered by what would hurt most if wrong.

**Q1 — THE BLOCKER-SHAPED QUESTION: what does a throw at `gdprService.ts:859` do to an erasure
that has already deleted data?** That call is step 12 of the erasure implementation and its
return was previously **ignored**. An Article 17 erasure that used to complete — silently
failing only to record its status — may now **abort after the deletions have happened**.
Establish: is there a catch between `:859` and the caller? Does the DSR end in a state that
says the erasure did not happen when data is already gone? **A record that under-states a
completed erasure is a compliance problem in the opposite direction from the one being fixed.**
Drive it; do not reason about it.

**Q2 — What did the FK protect, and who relied on it?** After this, nothing guarantees
`processed_by` names a real user. The author argues that is correct because the column holds
actor strings. **Enumerate the consumers** — anything that JOINs on it, resolves it to a user,
or assumes referential integrity — and say what breaks for a `connector:abc` value.
**This is the same shape as the finding that made #912 a NO GO: a consumer silently depending
on an invariant the change removes.** Look for it here before deciding.

**Q3 — Is the route's new behaviour actually what the author claims?** The commit says the
throw turns the route's misleading `200 {success:false}` into "the 500 its own catch already
handles". **Verify the catch exists and produces a 500** — and check whether any client
treats a 500 differently from a 200-with-false in a way that matters.

**Q4 — The migration, both provisioning paths.** `048` must be idempotent (`DROP CONSTRAINT
IF EXISTS` is claimed to be required, not stylistic, because two paths reach this table).
Prove: `048` twice on a migration-chain database, and once on a database built from the edited
azure init. Both must end `text` and accept a `connector:abc` INSERT. **The author claims to
have done exactly this — re-derive it rather than accepting it.**

**Q5 — `001_initial-schema.sql` is deliberately NOT touched.** The reasoning (an applied
historical record; the runner keys on filename with no checksum) is sound on its face.
**Confirm the END STATE matches** between a fresh replay through 048 and the edited init
files — the paths differ by design, the destination must not.
