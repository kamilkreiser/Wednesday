- G21-F5:
  - Keystrokes (inputType insertText or delete*) through empty or a too-low figure turn custom pricing off quietly, with no alert, and the figure is judged on change (blur or Enter). Too high is said at once, because more digits only raise it. A paste, a drop or a synthetic event counts as committed.
  - The rep's own choice of custom print mode is remembered (cpModeWanted) and comes back when a valid price is typed. It is cleared by Reset, reopen, and the F1 re-check.
- G21-F8: when a field holds a bound's own shown figure, to no more decimal places than that field shows (the /day max A$10.1918), it is taken as that bound.
