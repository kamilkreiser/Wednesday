TICKET: VSP-66 (gate 9 VSP65-O1, Major, pre-existing). A link death while a pool.connect() caller holds its client raised an uncaughtException and the portal exited.

CAUSE: pg-pool attaches its 'error' listener only while a client is IDLE, and removes it at checkout. IO1-O3's pool.on('error') covers the idle case. For a held client nothing listened.

FIX (gate 9's shape): server/db.js guard(), which every checkout passes through (pool.connect callers and pool.query alike), adds an 'error' listener at checkout and removes it on release.
- It logs one line per death, "[db] a held client lost its connection (discarded on release): <msg>". pg emits twice for one death (the FATAL, then the unexpected end), so the second emit is not logged.
- It does not swallow the failure for the holder: pg rejects the running and next queries.
- The dead client is not reused: pg sets _queryable=false, and pg-pool's _release discards such a client even on a no-error release.
- None of the six callers changed.
