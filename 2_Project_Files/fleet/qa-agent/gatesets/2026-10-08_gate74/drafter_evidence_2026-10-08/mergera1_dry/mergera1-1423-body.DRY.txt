KS-1164: assert the k6 status-code breakdown prints locale-grouped counts

Adds the locale-count coverage KS 1164 left behind. Test-only: no product code
changes, and `systemTest/performance/gate/report.ts` stays blob `ac59af37cbc6`.

`formatStatusCodeBreakdown` prints each k6 Rate counter through `toLocaleString()`,
so a 5xx count reads `1,234` rather than `1234`. Nothing asserted it — the grouping
could be deleted and every suite would stay green.

`--summary` does not end in `-summary.json`) was already fixed and merged as
`f3bf8698d628`, PR #1271. This PR does not touch it and does not close the ticket.
