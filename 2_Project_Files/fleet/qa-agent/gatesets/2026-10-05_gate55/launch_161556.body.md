## What this changes

`GET /api/delegations/{id}` published a contract that did not match its handler. The spec declared a
**bare `Delegation` record** as the 200 body; the handler (`routes/delegations.ts`, `GET /:id`)
answers `{ success, data: { delegation, chain } }`, the `chain` being whatever
`buildDelegationChain()` returns. Schemathesis flagged the 200 as `response_schema_conformance` in
the sweep of **2026-09-29**.

**The spec now follows the runtime**, not the other way round. `transfer.openapi.ts` registers the
200 content as a `successEnvelope(...)` whose `data` carries the **unchanged** `Delegation`
component by `$ref` alongside the chain object. `invalidReason` is declared optional because the
chain only carries it when `isValid` is false.

**This NARROWS KS-1015; it does not close it.** The ticket is a sweep finding covering **28
check/operation pairs with no live owner**. This PR owns **one**. The other 27 stay open on the
ticket, which is why the trailer below is `Refs`, not a closing keyword.
