## What changed

A **pure insertion** of 14 lines (`numstat 14 0`) after `is_baselined()` and **before**

artefact contents, no environment.

**A named side effect:** `jq -e .` takes its exit status from the output **value**, not only from
parseability, so a valid JSON `null` or `false` also reads as unreadable. That is the chosen
behaviour, stated here rather than discovered later.
