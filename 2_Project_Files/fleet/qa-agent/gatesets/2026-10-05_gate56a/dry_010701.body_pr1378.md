## Why `2027-01-01` and not the 31 Dec he named

`isLapsed` is `expires <= utcToday()` (`baseline-contract.mjs:141`), so a row is dead **on** its own
expires date. `2026-12-31` would lapse at 00:00Z on 31 Dec and **not cover the day he ruled**. He
was asked which he meant and answered: *"Valid through 31 Dec -> write 2027-01-01"*. It is the same
convention the superseded note in this file used when it wrote `2026-10-19` to mean "the end of
Sunday 18 Oct".
