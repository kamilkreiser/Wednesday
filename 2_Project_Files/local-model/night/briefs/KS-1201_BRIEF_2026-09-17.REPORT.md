## Harness findings (IMPROVEMENTS candidates; nothing built)

1. **B6 leaks the background children of a sibling suite.** `run_suite` runs `bash <suite>` and never reaps processes that outlive it. Here that is 4 stubs per checker run, 8 with a retry.
   - Candidate: run each suite in its own process group or session, and SIGTERM survivors whose argv carries `$CLONE` after each `run_suite`. Or snapshot `ps` before and after and name new ppid-1 children in the verdict.
2. **B6 picks the product ITSELF as a sibling** when the product is a `*.test.sh` that names its own basename (a `Usage:` line). The checker then runs the subject suite with its real dependencies (`npx tsx`: registry contact, 70 s per call under a network deny).
   - Candidate: exclude `PRODUCT` from `SIBS`, or say in the verdict that the sibling is the subject.
3. **`run_suite`'s timeout path crashes.**
   - Measured at 08:30:34: `subprocess.TimeoutExpired.stdout` is **bytes** (`b'hi\n'`) even with `text=True`. So `(e.stdout or "") + "\n"` raises TypeError whenever a timed-out suite printed anything.
   - The helper then prints nothing. B4 would read `rc=` as empty and fall into "not red"; B6 would read `fail_lines` as 0.
   - Candidate: decode bytes before concatenating.
   - Also, on timeout only `bash` is killed, not its children (the same class as finding 1).
4. **A sandboxed checker run can make a process-count cell vacuous.** `sandbox-exec` refuses setuid `/bin/ps`, so `ps` fails, the count reads 0, and every variant goes green. B4 would catch it here as "not red at the tip".
   - Candidate convention for any census cell: a reach term that proves the table was read (this brief's `PS_SEES_SELF`). For sandbox profiles: `(allow process-exec (literal "/bin/ps") (with no-sandbox))`.
5. **`npm_config_offline=true` as a checker environment default** for tiers that may run `npx` against a cache-only tool: 0.7 s against 70 s under a network deny, and no registry contact. Not measured networked.
