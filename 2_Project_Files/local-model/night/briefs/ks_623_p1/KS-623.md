## What is wrong (one paragraph)

`parseTestToken` (`authenticate.ts:19`) is the gate for the unsigned `test_token_<base64>` credential, whose payload self-asserts `sub`, `role` and `organizationId`. After the `ENABLE_TEST_TOKENS` opt-in (`:25`), its environment check is `if (process.env.NODE_ENV === 'production') return null;` (`:21`): a DENYLIST, so an unset `NODE_ENV`, `staging`, or a typo is treated as safe and the token is accepted. The gateway's twin refuses everything except `development` and `test` (`api-gateway/src/middleware/auth.ts:104-120`), so on such a deployment the two services disagree and the one that says yes is the auth service. The fix is the ticket's own: refuse unless `NODE_ENV` is exactly `development` or `test`.
