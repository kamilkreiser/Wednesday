## What is wrong
`links_jq` needs a line that STARTS with `ln`, a flag word with an `s`, and ends with `bin/jq`, an optional quote, then end of line or one of `;&|#`.
F-2078-1 (fails closed): legitimate spellings are judged "no link": `ln -sf "$JQ_BIN" "$root/bin/jq" 2>/dev/null`, `... >/dev/null`, `mkdir -p "$root/bin" && ln -sf ...`, `if ! ln -sf ...; then`, `( ln -sf ... )`, `command ln -sf ...`, `ln --symbolic --force ...`, and a backslash-continued `ln -sf \` + next line.
F-2078-2 (false ACCEPT): a trailing comment or word naming bin/jq passes: `ln -sf "$DOCKER_BIN" "$root/bin/docker"  # jq is at $root/bin/jq`, `ln -sf "$DOCKER_BIN" "$root/bin/docker"; echo "$root/bin/jq"`, `ln -sf "$root/bin/jq" "$stub/elsewhere" # bin/jq`.

Add two count cells and one continuation cell. Every existing cell keeps its label and stays green.
