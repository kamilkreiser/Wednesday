## What is wrong (one paragraph)
In `packages/shared/src/security/ssrf-guard.ts`, `safeOutboundRequest` (line 465) awaits
`resolvePublicAddresses(url.hostname)` at line 474 BEFORE the deadline is armed (`timeoutMs` is read at line 484 and
the `setTimeout` at line 560 covers only connect, transfer and drain). A DNS lookup that never calls back leaves the
whole call pending past its declared `timeoutMs`. Fix: arm the budget before resolution — race the lookup against
the deadline, and carry the REMAINDER of the budget into the request timer — so `timeoutMs` bounds the operation end
to end. A resolution that runs out of budget is reported like any other resolution failure
(`reason: 'blocked'` via the existing line 475).
