## What is wrong (one paragraph)

`step()` reads the header's denominator at `:225` as `_hdr_total="${1#*/}"; _hdr_total="${_hdr_total%%[!0-9]*}"`: the text after the slash, cut at the first non-digit. When that text is empty or starts with a non-digit (`3/`, `3/x`), `_hdr_total` is empty, the compare at `:226` aborts (correct), but every line of the abort message prints only the empty parsed value, so `3/x` reads as `3/` and the operator cannot see what the header actually said. The fix keeps the raw text in its own variable (`_hdr_tail`) at `:225`, parses the digits from it exactly as before, and appends it, quoted, to the bracketed line at `:231`. Separately, no cell exercised the empty and non-digit aborts, a bracket after the right total, or a total containing 0; the gate's mutants N7, N5 and N4 survived for that reason.
