## What is wrong (one paragraph)

In demo-service `POST /demo-api/persona/switch` (`src/routes/persona.ts`), the handler copies the auth service's httpOnly refresh-token cookie onto the response with `res.setHeader('Set-Cookie', ...)` (line 64) and only then builds the success answer with `res.json(...)` (line 67). If anything throws between, for example the answer cannot be serialised, the catch (line 78) answers the generic 500 and that 500 still carries the cookie. The fix records that the cookie was copied and removes it in the catch before the 500. Nothing else changes: the log line and the 500 body are untouched.
