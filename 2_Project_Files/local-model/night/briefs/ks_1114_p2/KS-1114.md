## What is wrong (one paragraph)

The title strategy (`POST /api/verification/verify` with only a `title`, `:1453-1470`) keeps draft and revoked documents out of its existence answer with `AND d.status NOT IN ('draft', 'revoked')` (`:1466`). That is an exact, case-sensitive deny-list, so a document whose `documents.status` is `deleted` or `expired`, or `REVOKED`, `Draft`, `revoked ` (the column is `VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL` with no CHECK), is answered as a HIT by title. The fix replaces that line with `AND lower(btrim(d.status)) NOT IN ('draft', 'revoked', 'deleted', 'expired')` and says so in the comment above. The 200 status, the hit body, the miss body, the tenant predicate, the erasure-sentinel guard and `LIMIT 1` do not change.
