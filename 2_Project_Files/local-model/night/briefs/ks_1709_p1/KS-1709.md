## What is wrong (one paragraph)

In `POST /api/certifications/issue` the route posts the certification's anchor to anchoring's `POST /api/anchors` (`:598-617`) and then checks only `if (upstream.ok) { ... }` (`:618-623`). There is no `else`. A refusal (HTTP 4xx or 5xx) is an answer, not an exception, so the `catch` at `:624` never sees it, and nothing is logged: on kintsugi on 2026-10-21 anchoring logged a bare 400 for Stuart's certification and originate logged nothing at all. The fix: after the `if` block's last line (`:622`, the `logger.info('Certification queued for anchoring', ...)` line), close the `if` with `} else {`, read the body as text (never throwing), and `logger.warn` one line naming the certification id, the status and the first 200 characters of the body. The existing closing `}` at `:623` then closes the `else`. Nothing else in the function changes.
