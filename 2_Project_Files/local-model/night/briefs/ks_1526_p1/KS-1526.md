## What is wrong (one paragraph)

`utils/logger.ts` builds the logger at `:37-45` with a top-level format of `errors({ stack: true })` and `timestamp(...)` only: nothing renders the entry into the string a transport writes. The Console transport has its own format (`:24-26`, `combine(json())` in production), so the console is fine. The two `winston.transports.File` transports pushed under `if (isProduction)` at `:32` and `:33` have none, so they write `undefined` for every log call and `logs/error.log` and `logs/combined.log` carry no information. The fix gives each of the two transports `format: combine(json())` (`combine` and `json` are already destructured from `winston.format` at `:11`), placed between the `level`/`filename` option and `maxsize`. Everything else on both lines is unchanged.
